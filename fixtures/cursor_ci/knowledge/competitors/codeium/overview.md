Codeium (now Windsurf) is an AI-native IDE and coding assistant built on proprietary models. Its free tier offers unlimited autocomplete and chat, undercutting GitHub Copilot's paid-only model. Paid plans (Individual $15/mo, Teams $30/user/mo, Enterprise custom) add advanced models, admin controls, and self-hosting. Key strengths: fast context-aware completions, deep codebase indexing, multi-file editing via 'Cascade,' and strong VS Code/JetBrains integration. Weaknesses: smaller ecosystem than Copilot, occasional latency on large repos, and less mature plugin marketplace. Market perception (2024-2025) positions it as a top Copilot alternative with superior free-tier value and rapid innovation, though Cursor leads on agentic editing mindshare. G2 and Reddit reviewers praise its speed and accuracy but note enterprise features lag behind Microsoft's offering.
