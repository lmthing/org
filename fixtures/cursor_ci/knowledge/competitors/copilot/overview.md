GitHub Copilot (Microsoft) leads the AI coding assistant market with deep VS Code/GitHub integration. In 2024-2025, it introduced a free tier (2,000 completions/month), Copilot Chat, agent mode, and multi-model support (GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini). Pricing: Individual $10/mo, Business $19/user/mo, Enterprise $39/user/mo. Strengths: best-in-class IDE integration, massive ecosystem, strong code completion quality, and enterprise compliance. Weaknesses: weaker multi-file refactoring vs Cursor, limited project-wide context, and higher latency on complex tasks. Market perception: dominant incumbent but facing pressure from Cursor (superior UX/context), Windsurf, and open-source alternatives. Users praise reliability but note it lags in agentic capabilities and cross-file editing. Microsoft's bundling strategy (Copilot + GitHub + Azure) creates stickiness that pure-play competitors struggle to match.
