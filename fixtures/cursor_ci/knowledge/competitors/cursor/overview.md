Cursor, by Anysphere (backed by $60M+ Series A at ~$400M valuation), is a VS Code-forked AI IDE launched 2023. By early 2025 it's widely considered the leading AI-native editor, praised for its Tab (next-edit prediction), agentic Composer with multi-file editing, and deep codebase context via indexing. Pricing: free Hobby tier, Pro at $20/month (500 fast premium requests), and usage-based Business plans. Strengths: best-in-class inline completions, fast iteration, strong developer love on Reddit/HN. Weaknesses: VS Code extension lag, occasional context-window limits, and rising competition from GitHub Copilot's agent mode (free tier launched Dec 2024) and Windsurf/Codium. Market perception: Cursor leads in capability but faces pricing pressure and platform risk as Microsoft integrates Copilot natively. Still the preferred tool for power users in 2025.
