# Indian Sauce Techniques

Indian sauce techniques center on building layers of flavor through sequential cooking methods. Key sauce families include: (1) Tadka/Baghaar — a hot oil tempering of whole spices poured over finished dishes; (2) Masala-based gravies — onion-tomato-ginger-garlic pastes cooked down with ground spices, forming the backbone of curries like tikka masala, butter chicken, and korma; (3) Coconut-based sauces — common in South Indian cuisine, using fresh coconut, coconut milk, or coconut paste with curry leaves and mustard seeds; (4) Yogurt/nut-based sauces — creamy sauces thickened with yogurt, cashew, almond, or poppy seed paste (e.g., korma, pasanda).

Core technique steps: (1) Tadka/Tempering — heat ghee or oil, add whole spices (cumin seeds, mustard seeds, dried chilies, curry leaves) until they crackle and release aromatics; (2) Bhunai/Sautéing — cook onion paste until deeply browned (15-20 min), then add ginger-garlic paste and cook until raw smell disappears; (3) Tomato integration — add tomato puree and cook until oil separates from the masala, indicating proper doneness; (4) Spice blooming — add ground spices (turmeric, cumin, coriander, garam masala, chili powder) to the hot fat and cook briefly to activate fat-soluble compounds; (5) Simmering — add liquid (water, stock, coconut milk, cream) and protein/vegetables, simmer until tender and flavors meld; (6) Finishing — final tadka, kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves), fresh cilantro, or a splash of cream.

Essential ingredients: Ghee or neutral oil; whole spices (cumin seeds, mustard seeds, cinnamon stick, cardamom pods, cloves, bay leaf, dried red chilies, curry leaves); aromatics (onion, ginger, garlic, green chilies); ground spices (turmeric, cumin, coriander, garam masala, Kashmiri red chili powder, amchur); tomato (fresh, puree, or paste); acid (yogurt, lemon juice, tamarind); finishing elements (kasuri methi, cilantro, cream, butter).

Representative recipe — Basic Onion-Tomato Masala (yields ~2 cups): Heat 3 tbsp ghee/oil. Add 1 tsp cumin seeds, 2 green cardamom pods, 1-inch cinnamon stick, 2 cloves — let crackle. Add 2 cups finely chopped onion, cook 15 min until deep golden. Add 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, cook 2 min. Add 1.5 cups tomato puree, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp cumin powder, 1 tsp coriander powder, 1/2 tsp Kashmiri chili powder, salt. Cook 10-12 min until oil separates. Finish with 1/2 tsp garam masala and 1 tsp crushed kasuri methi. This base can be portioned and frozen, then customized with cream for butter masala, coconut milk for South Indian style, or yogurt for a tangier finish.
