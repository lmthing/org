## Common Tests and Procedures

- **CBC with Differential** -- quantitative blood cell assessment
- **Peripheral Blood Smear** -- morphology (schistocytes, spherocytes, sickle cells, blasts, teardrop cells)
- **Reticulocyte Count** -- marrow response (corrected retic, RPI)
- **Iron Studies (Fe, TIBC, Ferritin, Transferrin Saturation)** -- iron deficiency vs. anemia of chronic disease
- **B12 / Folate Levels** -- megaloblastic anemia evaluation
- **Hemoglobin Electrophoresis / HPLC** -- hemoglobinopathy identification (sickle cell, thalassemia)
- **DAT (Direct Coombs)** -- autoimmune hemolytic anemia
- **PT/INR, aPTT** -- coagulation pathway assessment
- **Mixing Study (1:1)** -- factor deficiency vs. inhibitor (aPTT correction)
- **Factor Assays (VIII, IX, VWF)** -- bleeding disorder workup
- **Bone Marrow Biopsy + Aspirate** -- hematologic malignancy, aplastic anemia, MDS
- **Flow Cytometry** -- immunophenotyping of leukemias and lymphomas
- **JAK2 V617F Mutation** -- myeloproliferative neoplasm (polycythemia vera, ET, MF)
- **ADAMTS13 Activity** -- TTP diagnosis (< 10% activity is diagnostic)

## Red-Flag Symptoms

- **Pentad of TTP: microangiopathic hemolysis + thrombocytopenia + neurologic symptoms + renal dysfunction + fever** -- plasma exchange emergently (do NOT give platelets)
- **Rapidly dropping hemoglobin + schistocytes on smear + elevated LDH** -- microangiopathic hemolytic anemia (TTP, HUS, DIC -- urgent plasma exchange if TTP)
- **Bleeding from multiple sites + prolonged PT/aPTT + low fibrinogen + elevated D-dimer** -- DIC (treat underlying cause, supportive transfusion)
- **WBC > 100,000 with blasts** -- leukocytosis emergency (tumor lysis prophylaxis, leukapheresis if symptomatic)
- **Platelets < 10,000 with active bleeding** -- emergency platelet transfusion (ITP: IVIG + steroids first line)
- **New-onset DVT in unusual site (mesenteric, cerebral, hepatic)** -- consider inherited thrombophilia, paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria (PNH), or occult malignancy
- **Hyperviscosity symptoms (headache, blurred vision, confusion) + IgM paraprotein** -- Waldenstrom macroglobulinemia (plasmapheresis)
- **Sickle cell crisis with acute chest syndrome** -- fever + chest pain + new infiltrate + hypoxia (exchange transfusion, antibiotics)
- **Pancytopenia with hypocellular marrow** -- aplastic anemia (urgent referral for immunosuppressive therapy or transplant)
- **New leukocytosis with circulating blasts + cytopenias** -- acute leukemia (urgent bone marrow biopsy, cytogenetics, flow cytometry)
