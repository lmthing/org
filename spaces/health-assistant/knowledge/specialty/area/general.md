# General Practice / Internal Medicine

## Key Terms and Abbreviations

| Term | Abbreviation | Meaning |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Chief Complaint | CC | Primary reason for the visit |
| History of Present Illness | HPI | Detailed narrative of the current problem |
| Review of Systems | ROS | Systematic inquiry across organ systems |
| Physical Examination | PE | Hands-on clinical assessment |
| Differential Diagnosis | DDx | List of possible conditions ranked by likelihood |
| Point of Maximum Impulse | PMI | Location of strongest heartbeat on chest wall |
| Activities of Daily Living | ADLs | Basic self-care tasks |
| Instrumental ADLs | IADLs | Complex daily tasks (cooking, finances, meds) |
| Nothing By Mouth | NPO | Nil per os |
| As Needed | PRN | Pro re nata |
| Twice Daily | BID | Bis in die |
| Three Times Daily | TID | Ter in die |
| Four Times Daily | QID | Quater in die |

## Common Tests and Procedures

- **Complete Blood Count (CBC)** -- screening for anemia, infection, thrombocytopenia
- **Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP)** -- electrolytes, glucose, renal function, liver enzymes
- **Lipid Panel** -- total cholesterol, LDL, HDL, triglycerides
- **HbA1c** -- 3-month average blood glucose
- **TSH** -- thyroid screening
- **Urinalysis (UA)** -- infection, hematuria, proteinuria
- **Electrocardiogram (ECG/EKG)** -- arrhythmia, ischemia screening
- **Chest X-Ray (CXR)** -- cardiopulmonary screening
- **Fecal Immunochemical Test (FIT)** -- colorectal cancer screening
- **Pap Smear** -- cervical cancer screening

## Red-Flag Symptoms

- **Unexplained weight loss** > 10 lbs in 6 months -- malignancy, hyperthyroidism, TB
- **Night sweats** drenching -- lymphoma, TB, endocarditis
- **New-onset seizure** in adults -- intracranial mass, stroke, metabolic derangement
- **Sudden severe headache** ("thunderclap") -- subarachnoid hemorrhage
- **Chest pain with dyspnea** -- ACS, PE, aortic dissection
- **Progressive dysphagia** -- esophageal cancer, stricture, achalasia
- **Hematuria without pain** -- bladder or renal cell carcinoma
- **Unexplained fever > 101 F for > 3 weeks** -- FUO workup indicated
- **New focal neurological deficit** -- stroke, intracranial mass
- **Syncope with exertion** -- aortic stenosis, HCM, arrhythmia
