# Cardiology

## Key Terms and Abbreviations

| Term | Abbreviation | Meaning |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Acute Coronary Syndrome | ACS | Unstable angina, NSTEMI, STEMI |
| Atrial Fibrillation | AFib / AF | Irregular supraventricular arrhythmia |
| Congestive Heart Failure | CHF | Pump failure with volume overload |
| Myocardial Infarction | MI | Heart attack |
| Percutaneous Coronary Intervention | PCI | Angioplasty with stent |
| Left Ventricular Ejection Fraction | LVEF | Pump efficiency percentage |
| Deep Vein Thrombosis | DVT | Venous clot, usually leg |
| Pulmonary Embolism | PE | Lung clot from venous source |
| Troponin | cTnI / cTnT | Cardiac injury biomarker |
| BNP / NT-proBNP | BNP | Heart failure biomarker |
| QTc | QTc | Corrected QT interval |
| ST elevation | STE | Acute injury pattern on ECG |

## Common Tests and Procedures

- **Electrocardiogram (ECG)** -- 12-lead resting; arrhythmia, ischemia, chamber enlargement
- **Echocardiogram (TTE/TEE)** -- LVEF, valve function, wall motion, pericardial effusion
- **Cardiac Stress Test** -- exercise or pharmacologic; ischemia detection
- **Coronary Angiography** -- gold standard for coronary artery visualization
- **Cardiac MRI** -- myocardial viability, infiltrative disease, pericardial disease
- **Holter Monitor (24-48 hr)** -- ambulatory arrhythmia detection
- **Event Monitor (30 days)** -- intermittent arrhythmia capture
- **BNP / NT-proBNP** -- heart failure severity and monitoring
- **Cardiac Catheterization** -- hemodynamic measurements, coronary anatomy
- **CT Coronary Angiogram** -- non-invasive coronary artery assessment

## Red-Flag Symptoms

- **Crushing substernal chest pain** radiating to jaw/arm -- STEMI until proven otherwise
- **Sudden-onset dyspnea** with pleuritic pain and hemoptysis -- pulmonary embolism
- **Syncope during exertion** -- aortic stenosis, HCM, ventricular arrhythmia
- **New wide-complex tachycardia** -- ventricular tachycardia until proven otherwise
- **Sudden severe tearing chest/back pain** -- aortic dissection (type A is surgical emergency)
- **New-onset AFib with RVR** (> 150 bpm) -- hemodynamic instability risk
- **Jugular venous distension + muffled heart sounds + hypotension** -- cardiac tamponade (Beck triad)
- **Bilateral lower extremity edema with rising JVP** -- decompensated heart failure
- **New murmur with fever** -- infective endocarditis
- **ST elevation in contiguous leads** -- activate cath lab for emergent PCI
