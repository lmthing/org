## Common Tests and Procedures

- **Newborn Screening (heel stick)** -- metabolic, endocrine, hemoglobinopathy screening (varies by state)
- **Apgar Score** -- 0--10 at 1 and 5 minutes (Appearance, Pulse, Grimace, Activity, Respiration)
- **Developmental Screening (ASQ-3, M-CHAT-R/F)** -- autism and developmental delay screening
- **Growth Chart Plotting** -- WHO (0--2 yr), CDC (2--20 yr) height, weight, BMI, head circumference
- **Lead Level Screening** -- risk-based or universal at 12 and 24 months
- **Hemoglobin / Hematocrit** -- anemia screening at 12 months
- **Vision Screening** -- starting at age 3, then annually
- **Hearing Screening (OAE / ABR)** -- newborn, then at-risk intervals
- **Bilirubin Level (Total/Direct)** -- neonatal jaundice assessment
- **Rapid Strep / Throat Culture** -- pharyngitis in children > 3 years
- **Chest X-Ray** -- pneumonia, foreign body, cardiac silhouette
- **Abdominal Ultrasound** -- pyloric stenosis, intussusception, appendicitis

## Red-Flag Symptoms

- **Febrile infant < 28 days with temp >= 100.4 F (38 C)** -- full sepsis workup (CBC, blood culture, UA/UCx, LP), empiric antibiotics regardless of appearance
- **Ill-appearing febrile infant 29--60 days** -- high-risk: sepsis workup + empiric antibiotics; low-risk per Rochester criteria may observe
- **Bilious vomiting in newborn** -- intestinal malrotation with midgut volvulus until proven (urgent upper GI series, surgical emergency)
- **Projectile non-bilious vomiting in 2--8 week old** -- pyloric stenosis (palpable olive, ultrasound, metabolic alkalosis)
- **Intermittent severe abdominal pain + drawing up legs + bloody stool** -- intussusception (target sign on ultrasound, air enema reduction)
- **Respiratory distress in infant** -- nasal flaring, grunting, retractions, tachypnea > 60 (assess for bronchiolitis, pneumonia, CHD)
- **Blue episode / ALTE (apparent life-threatening event)** -- apnea, color change, tone change (rule out seizure, sepsis, cardiac, abuse)
- **Bruising in non-mobile infant** -- non-accidental trauma must be considered (skeletal survey, head CT, LFTs, lipase, coagulation panel)
- **New onset seizure with fever in child 6 months -- 5 years** -- febrile seizure (simple vs. complex), rule out meningitis if no clear source
- **Acute onset limp or refusal to bear weight in child** -- septic arthritis, transient synovitis, osteomyelitis, fracture (MRI if septic arthritis suspected)
