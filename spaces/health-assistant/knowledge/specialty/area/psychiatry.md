# Psychiatry

## Key Terms and Abbreviations

| Term | Abbreviation | Meaning |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Major Depressive Disorder | MDD | Persistent depressed mood/anhedonia >= 2 weeks |
| Generalized Anxiety Disorder | GAD | Excessive worry >= 6 months |
| Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder | PTSD | Trauma re-experiencing, avoidance, hyperarousal |
| Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder | OCD | Intrusive thoughts + compulsive behaviors |
| Bipolar Affective Disorder | BPAD | Mania/hypomania alternating with depression |
| Schizophrenia | SCZ | Psychotic disorder >= 6 months |
| Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder | ADHD | Inattention and/or hyperactivity-impulsivity |
| Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitor | SSRI | First-line antidepressant class |
| Serotonin-Norepinephrine Reuptake Inhibitor | SNRI | Antidepressant/anxiolytic class |
| Cognitive Behavioral Therapy | CBT | Evidence-based psychotherapy |
| Patient Health Questionnaire-9 | PHQ-9 | Depression severity screening |
| Generalized Anxiety Disorder-7 | GAD-7 | Anxiety severity screening |
| Columbia Suicide Severity Rating Scale | C-SSRS | Suicide risk assessment |
| Mini-Mental State Examination | MMSE | Cognitive screening |
| Serotonin Syndrome | -- | Life-threatening serotonergic excess |
| Neuroleptic Malignant Syndrome | NMS | Life-threatening antipsychotic adverse reaction |

## Common Tests and Procedures

- **Psychiatric Interview (MSE -- Mental Status Examination)** -- appearance, behavior, speech, mood, affect, thought process, thought content, cognition, insight, judgment
- **PHQ-9** -- depression screening (0--4 minimal, 5--9 mild, 10--14 moderate, 15--19 mod-severe, 20--27 severe)
- **GAD-7** -- anxiety screening (0--4 minimal, 5--9 mild, 10--14 moderate, 15--21 severe)
- **C-SSRS** -- suicidal ideation and behavior assessment
- **AUDIT-C** -- alcohol use disorder screening
- **MoCA / MMSE** -- cognitive screening (MoCA more sensitive for mild cognitive impairment)
- **Thyroid Function (TSH, FT4)** -- rule out medical cause of psychiatric symptoms
- **Vitamin B12 / Folate** -- rule out deficiency in depression/cognitive changes
- **Drug Screen (urine)** -- substance use identification
- **Lithium Level** -- therapeutic drug monitoring (0.6--1.2 mEq/L)
- **Valproic Acid Level** -- therapeutic drug monitoring (50--100 mcg/mL)
- **Clozapine: ANC monitoring** -- weekly to monthly per REMS program

## Red-Flag Symptoms

- **Active suicidal ideation with plan and intent** -- psychiatric emergency (C-SSRS, safety planning, consider hospitalization)
- **Command auditory hallucinations to harm self or others** -- psychotic emergency (antipsychotic initiation, safety assessment)
- **Serotonin syndrome** -- agitation, hyperreflexia, clonus, hyperthermia (> 101 F), diaphoresis after serotonergic medication change (discontinue offending agents, cyproheptadine)
- **Neuroleptic malignant syndrome** -- rigidity, hyperthermia (> 104 F), altered mental status, autonomic instability after antipsychotic (dantrolene, bromocriptine, stop antipsychotic)
- **Acute psychosis with dangerous behavior** -- emergency psychiatric evaluation, involuntary hold if danger to self/others
- **Severe mania with psychosis** -- hospitalization, mood stabilizer + antipsychotic
- **Acute alcohol withdrawal with DTs** -- tremor, hallucinations, autonomic hyperactivity, seizures (benzodiazepine-based protocol, CIWA monitoring)
- **Catatonia** -- mutism, posturing, waxy flexibility (lorazepam challenge, consider ECT)
- **First episode of psychosis in young person** -- rule out substance-induced, organic causes; early intervention critical
- **Domestic violence or abuse disclosed** -- safety assessment, mandatory reporting if applicable, resource referral
