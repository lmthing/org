# Rheumatology

## Key Terms and Abbreviations

| Term | Abbreviation | Meaning |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Rheumatoid Arthritis | RA | Autoimmune erosive polyarthritis |
| Systemic Lupus Erythematosus | SLE | Multi-system autoimmune disease |
| Sjogren Syndrome | SS | Exocrine gland autoimmune disease |
| Systemic Sclerosis | SSc | Scleroderma, skin + organ fibrosis |
| Ankylosing Spondylitis | AS | Axial spondyloarthritis |
| Psoriatic Arthritis | PsA | Arthritis associated with psoriasis |
| Gout | -- | Monosodium urate crystal arthritis |
| Pseudogout (CPPD) | -- | Calcium pyrophosphate crystal arthritis |
| Giant Cell Arteritis | GCA | Large vessel vasculitis (temporal arteritis) |
| Anti-Neutrophil Cytoplasmic Antibody | ANCA | Small vessel vasculitis marker |
| Disease-Modifying Anti-Rheumatic Drug | DMARD | Methotrexate, sulfasalazine, leflunomide |
| Biologic DMARD | bDMARD | TNF inhibitors, IL-6 inhibitors, B-cell depleters |
| C-Reactive Protein | CRP | Acute phase reactant for disease activity |
| Erythrocyte Sedimentation Rate | ESR | Chronic inflammation marker |
| Janus Kinase Inhibitor | JAKi | Tofacitinib, baricitinib, upadacitinib |

## Common Tests and Procedures

- **Joint Aspiration (arthrocentesis)** -- crystal analysis, cell count, culture (septic vs. inflammatory vs. crystal)
- **Rheumatoid Factor (RF)** -- RA screening (low specificity, also in Sjogren, HBV, endocarditis)
- **Anti-CCP (cyclic citrullinated peptide)** -- RA (high specificity > 95%)
- **ANA (antinuclear antibody)** -- autoimmune screening (positive in SLE, SSc, Sjogren, MCTD)
- **Anti-dsDNA, Anti-Sm** -- SLE-specific antibodies
- **Anti-Ro/SS-A, Anti-La/SS-B** -- Sjogren syndrome, subacute cutaneous lupus
- **ANCA (p-ANCA/c-ANCA, MPO/PR3)** -- vasculitis (GPA, EGPA, MPA)
- **Complement Levels (C3, C4, CH50)** -- low in active SLE, cryoglobulinemia
- **HLA-B27** -- axial spondyloarthritis association (present in ~90% of AS)
- **ACE Level** -- sarcoidosis (low specificity)
- **Muscle Biopsy** -- myositis diagnosis
- **Schirmer Test** -- sicca assessment (Sjogren)
- **X-Ray (hands, feet, SI joints)** -- erosions, joint space narrowing, sacroiliitis

## Red-Flag Symptoms

- **Unilateral temporal headache + jaw claudication + vision change in > 50 yr** -- giant cell arteritis (start high-dose prednisone immediately, do NOT wait for biopsy; temporal artery biopsy within 2 weeks)
- **Fever + new heart murmur + positive ANCA** -- endocarditis mimicking vasculitis (blood cultures before antibiotics)
- **Acute monoarthritis + fever** -- septic arthritis until culture proven negative (emergent arthrocentesis)
- **Rapidly progressive glomerulonephritis + hemoptysis** -- ANCA-associated vasculitis with pulmonary capillaritis (urgent plasmapheresis + cyclophosphamide/rituximab)
- **Scleroderma renal crisis** -- new hypertension + rising creatinine + microangiopathic hemolysis (ACE inhibitor immediately)
- **New-onset Raynaud + puffy fingers + positive ANA** -- early systemic sclerosis (nailfold capillaroscopy, pulmonary function testing)
- **Proximal muscle weakness + elevated CK + dysphagia** -- inflammatory myopathy (dermatomyositis has malignancy association, age-appropriate cancer screening)
- **SLE patient: fever + decreasing complement + active urine sediment** -- lupus nephritis flare (kidney biopsy for classification, ISN/RPS class)
- **Acute febrile illness + very high ferritin (> 10,000)** -- adult-onset Still disease or hemophagocytic syndrome (MAS)
- **Mechanical back pain worsening with rest + morning stiffness > 30 min + age < 45** -- axial spondyloarthritis (MRI SI joints, HLA-B27)
