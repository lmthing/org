# World Health Organization (WHO)

- **Main URL:** https://www.who.int/
- **Health Topics:** https://www.who.int/health-topics
- **International Classification of Diseases (ICD):** https://icd.who.int/
- **Global Health Observatory:** https://www.who.int/data/gho

## What It Excels At

- Global health statistics, country profiles, and disease burden estimates
- International disease classification (ICD-10/ICD-11) coding standards
- Pandemic surveillance, health emergency alerts, and situation reports
- Essential medicines lists and immunization policy recommendations
- Public health guidelines for low- and middle-income country contexts

## Search Tips

- Use **Health Topics** for structured browsing by disease, risk factor, or public health theme
- Search the **Global Health Observatory** for downloadable data on mortality, morbidity, and health systems by country
- Access **ICD-11** browser for standardized diagnostic coding lookups
- Check **Disease Outbreak News (DONs)** for real-time alerts on emerging health threats
- Use the **Publications** section for WHO technical reports, guidelines, and statistical annexes
- Filter by WHO region (Africa, Americas, South-East Asia, Europe, Eastern Mediterranean, Western Pacific)

## Authority Level

**Tier 1 -- Highest.** The WHO is the directing and coordinating authority for health within the United Nations system. Content is produced by international panels of experts and subject to multi-stakeholder review. The recognized global authority for pandemic response, disease classification standards (ICD), and international health regulations.
