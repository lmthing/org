# PubMed

- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/
- **API:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/tools/oai/

## What It Excels At

- Peer-reviewed biomedical research articles and clinical studies
- Systematic reviews and meta-analyses
- Randomized controlled trials (RCTs)
- Preclinical and translational research
- Historical medical literature going back decades

## Search Tips

- Use **MeSH (Medical Subject Headings)** terms for precise searching: wrap terms in brackets, e.g., `[MeSH Terms]`
- Apply **Clinical Query filters** to limit results to therapy, diagnosis, prognosis, or etiology
- Use Boolean operators: `AND`, `OR`, `NOT` in uppercase
- Filter by publication date, article type (RCT, review, guideline), and species (human/animal)
- Use the **Advanced** builder for complex queries with field-specific tags like `[Title/Abstract]`
- Add `systematic[sb]` to find systematic reviews, or `randomized controlled trial[pt]` for RCTs
- Use **Related Articles** links to find similar studies from a known relevant paper
- Free full-text links via PubMed Central (PMC) are indicated with a "Free PMC article" badge

## Authority Level

**Tier 1 -- Highest.** Maintained by the National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI) at the U.S. National Library of Medicine. All content is peer-reviewed and indexed by professional indexers using controlled MeSH vocabulary. The gold standard for biomedical literature search.
