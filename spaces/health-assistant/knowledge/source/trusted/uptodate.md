# UpToDate

- **Main URL:** https://www.uptodate.com/
- **Topic Search:** Available via the main search bar on the homepage
- **Calculators:** https://www.uptodate.com/calculators
- **Practice-Changing Updates:** Highlighted on the homepage

## What It Excels At

- Point-of-care clinical decision support for diagnosis and treatment
- Comprehensive topic reviews synthesizing the latest evidence with expert recommendations
- Drug interaction and dosing calculators
- Graded recommendations (Grade 1/Grade 2, based on quality of evidence) following GRADE methodology
- Differential diagnosis pathways

## Search Tips

- Use the main search bar for condition, drug, or symptom-based queries
- Each topic review starts with a **"Summary and Recommendations"** section for quick clinical action
- Recommendations are tagged with **grade** (1A, 1B, 2A, 2B, etc.) indicating strength and evidence quality
- Use the **"Graphic"** tabs within topics for algorithms, tables, and images
- Check **"What's New"** for recently updated practice-changing content
- Use **patient information** handouts linked from topic reviews for patient education
- The **calculators** section includes clinical tools like Wells Score, CHA2DS2-VASc, and creatinine clearance

## Authority Level

**Tier 1 -- Highest (with paid access).** Content is authored and peer-reviewed by specialist physicians and updated continuously. UpToDate is the most widely used point-of-care clinical decision support tool in U.S. hospitals. Recommendations are explicitly graded using the GRADE system. Note: requires an institutional or personal subscription for full access.
