# American Academy of Family Physicians (AAFP)

- **Main URL:** https://www.aafp.org/
- **Clinical Recommendations:** https://www.aafp.org/family-physician/patient-care/clinical-recommendations.html
- **American Family Physician (AFP) Journal:** https://www.aafp.org/pubs/afp.html
- **FPM (Family Practice Management):** https://www.aafp.org/pubs/fpm.html

## What It Excels At

- Primary care clinical recommendations and preventive screening guidelines
- Evidence-based reviews tailored to family medicine and general practice
- CME (Continuing Medical Education) articles and board review resources
- Practice management tools and workflow optimization
- Patient education handouts in English and Spanish

## Search Tips

- Browse **Clinical Recommendations** by topic area (preventive care, chronic disease, acute conditions)
- The **AFP Journal** publishes structured reviews with key practice recommendations at the top of each article
- Use the **"Sort by Date"** option to find the most current recommendations
- AFP articles include **"Patient Information"** handouts that can be printed or shared digitally
- Check the **AAFP Screening Recommendations** table for age- and risk-based preventive care guidelines
- Search by **ICD-10 or CPT code** for billing and coding guidance tied to clinical topics

## Authority Level

**Tier 2 -- High.** Content is authored by practicing family physicians and reviewed by the AAFP's clinical editorial board. Guidelines align with USPSTF recommendations. The primary professional resource for family medicine practitioners in the U.S. Recommendations are practical and oriented toward real-world primary care settings.
