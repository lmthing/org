## Symptom Patterns (Schedule Appointment Within 1-2 Weeks)

### General
- Low-grade fever (<101°F/38.3°C) persisting >3 days
- Unexplained fatigue lasting >2 weeks
- Unintentional weight loss (>5% body weight in 1 month)
- New rash without systemic symptoms

### Chronic Condition Management
- Worsening of controlled chronic condition (e.g., blood sugar trending up, BP readings above target)
- Medication side effects that are bothersome but not dangerous
- Need for medication refill for chronic condition

### Musculoskeletal
- Joint pain with swelling (not traumatic, not red/hot)
- Back pain without red flags (no numbness, weakness, bowel/bladder changes)
- Gradual onset knee, hip, or shoulder pain

### Respiratory
- Chronic cough (>3 weeks) without red flags
- Mild persistent nasal congestion with facial pressure (possible sinusitis)
- Mild wheezing with known asthma (controlled with inhaler)

### Gastrointestinal
- Persistent heartburn >2x/week despite OTC treatment
- Intermittent mild abdominal pain without weight loss or blood
- Change in bowel habits (>2 weeks, no blood)

### Dermatological
- Changing mole (size, color, border, bleeding) — needs dermatology evaluation
- Persistent skin rash not responding to OTC treatment
- New skin growth of concern

### Mental Health
- Persistent low mood >2 weeks
- Increased anxiety affecting daily function
- Sleep disturbance >2 weeks

## Time-to-Treatment

- **Target: within 1-2 weeks**
- Schedule a primary care appointment
- If symptoms significantly worsen, escalate to urgent or emergency

## Care Setting

**Primary care appointment** — schedule at convenience within 1-2 weeks

## Red-Flag Escalation Criteria

Escalate to urgent if:
- Fever rises above 103°F/39.4°C
- Pain becomes severe
- New neurological symptoms develop
- Blood appears in stool, urine, or vomit
- Breathing becomes noticeably difficult
- Mental status changes
