## Core philosophy

Asian cuisines balance **five flavour dimensions**: salty, sweet, sour, bitter, and umami — simultaneously, not sequentially. The goal is harmony in a single bite, not a progressive tasting arc. Fresh aromatics (ginger, garlic, scallion, lemongrass, galangal) are the backbone; fermented condiments (fish sauce, soy, miso, doenjang) are the depth layer.

## Wok technique

The wok is a **tool of high heat and quick movement**, not a stockpot or skillet substitute.

- **Wok hei (镬气)**: the slightly charred, smoky flavour that comes from a superheated wok and rapid tossing. Requires a burner producing ≥ 80,000 BTU; home stoves rarely achieve full wok hei — work in smaller batches and keep the wok dry before adding oil.
- **Velveting**: marinate protein in cornstarch + egg white + soy sauce, then briefly blanch in water or oil before stir-frying. Creates silky, tender texture that resists overcooking.
- **Mise en place is mandatory**: stir-fries cook in 2–3 min total; everything must be prepped and staged before the wok heats.

## Umami building blocks

| Ingredient | Cuisine | Flavour note |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Soy sauce (light/dark) | Chinese, Japanese, Korean | Salt + umami; dark adds colour |
| Fish sauce | Thai, Vietnamese | Pungent umami; adds body to braises |
| Oyster sauce | Chinese | Sweet-savoury; thickens sauces |
| Miso (white/red/brown) | Japanese | Fermented depth; never boil it |
| Gochujang | Korean | Fermented chilli-soybean paste; complex heat |
| Shrimp paste (belacan) | Malaysian, Indonesian | Intense, funky; always cook before eating |
| Dashi (kombu + bonito) | Japanese | Clean, sea-mineral umami base |

## Regional distinctions

| Region | Defining flavours | Signature dishes |
|--------|------------------|-----------------|
| Cantonese (China) | Mild, delicate, ingredient-forward | Dim sum, steamed fish with ginger-scallion oil, char siu |
| Sichuan (China) | Numbing (málà: Sichuan pepper + chilli) | Mapo tofu, dan dan noodles, hot pot |
| Japanese | Umami precision, minimal fat, seasonal | Ramen, sushi, tonkatsu, okonomiyaki |
| Korean | Fermented depth, bold heat, sesame | Bibimbap, galbi, kimchi jjigae, japchae |
| Thai | Balance of hot, sour, sweet, salty | Pad Thai, green curry, som tum, larb |
| Vietnamese | Light, herb-forward, fish sauce base | Pho, banh mi, bun cha, spring rolls |

## Key technique notes

- **Aromatics first**: garlic and ginger burn in 30 seconds at wok heat. Add them right before the protein, not before oil.
- **Corn starch slurry**: thickens stir-fry sauces rapidly; add at the very end and toss 20 seconds only.
- **Rice washing**: remove surface starch to prevent gluey texture — rinse until water runs clear, then soak 30 min for better texture.
- **Dashi rule**: kombu goes in cold water, brought to 60°C (not boiling) and held 30 min; bonito flakes steep off-heat for 5 min then strained.
