## Definition

Vegan cooking contains **no animal products whatsoever**: no meat, fish, shellfish, dairy, eggs, honey, gelatin, or animal-derived additives (e.g. carmine, isinglass, casein, whey, lactose).

## Disqualifying ingredients (flag these)

| Ingredient | Category | Vegan substitute |
|-----------|----------|-----------------|
| Butter | Dairy fat | Vegan butter, coconut oil, or neutral oil |
| Cream / milk | Dairy | Oat milk, full-fat coconut milk, cashew cream |
| Cheese (any) | Dairy | Nutritional yeast for flavour; cashew-based cheeses for texture |
| Eggs (binder) | Eggs | Flax egg (1 tbsp ground flax + 3 tbsp water), chia egg, aquafaba |
| Eggs (structure/lift) | Eggs | Aquafaba (chickpea liquid, whips to stiff peaks), JUST Egg |
| Honey | Bee product | Maple syrup, agave, date syrup |
| Gelatin | Animal collagen | Agar-agar (1:1 by weight in most applications) |
| Worcestershire sauce | Anchovies | Vegan Worcestershire (e.g. Annie's), soy sauce + tamarind |
| Parmesan (DOP) | Dairy | Nutritional yeast + cashew powder + salt |
| Stock/broth | Often animal-based | Vegetable stock; miso-based; kombu dashi (kombu only — bonito is not vegan) |

## Hidden non-vegan traps

- **Refined white sugar**: some brands filter through bone char. Use raw cane sugar, coconut sugar, or certified vegan sugar.
- **Wine and beer**: fined with isinglass or egg whites. Look for vegan labelling.
- **Dark chocolate**: often contains milk solids. Check ingredients.
- **Pasta**: fresh egg pasta is not vegan; dried semolina pasta (durum + water) is vegan.
- **Bread**: many commercial breads contain milk powder, butter, or eggs.
- **Caesar dressing**: anchovies and Worcestershire. Always substitute or make from scratch.

## Protein and nutrition notes

A well-constructed vegan diet is nutritionally complete, but these require deliberate attention:

- **Protein**: combine varied sources — legumes (beans, lentils, chickpeas), tofu, tempeh, seitan, edamame, quinoa. Varied diet over the day provides complete amino acid profiles.
- **Vitamin B12**: not available from plant foods. Always flag to user that supplementation is needed for long-term vegan diets.
- **Iron**: non-haem iron from plants is less bioavailable. Pair iron-rich foods (lentils, spinach, fortified cereals) with vitamin C.
- **Omega-3**: flaxseed, chia seeds, hemp seeds, walnuts provide ALA. EPA/DHA from algae oil supplements.
- **Calcium**: fortified plant milks, tofu (set with calcium sulphate), kale, bok choy, almonds.

## Fit-check rules

A recipe **fails** the vegan check if any ingredient from the disqualifying list is present and no substitution is noted. Flag each non-compliant ingredient with its suggested substitute.
