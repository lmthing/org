## Politics Category

Covers government actions, elections, policy changes, diplomatic relations, legislation, and political campaigns.

### Classification signals

Keywords: government, election, vote, parliament, congress, senate, president, minister, policy, legislation, law, regulation, diplomacy, treaty, sanctions, campaign, polling, party, coalition, veto, bill, amendment

### Sub-topics

- Elections and campaigns
- Government policy and legislation
- International relations and diplomacy
- Domestic politics and party politics
- Judicial and legal developments
- Regulatory changes
- Political scandals and investigations

### Trusted sources for politics

Wire services first (Reuters, AP), then established newspapers of record (NYT, WaPo, Guardian, WSJ), public broadcasters (BBC, NPR, DW).

### Classification notes

- Distinguish between political analysis (opinion) and political reporting (facts)
- Be cautious with political polling data — always check sample size, margin of error, and methodology
- Political coverage is particularly prone to framing bias — cross-reference across outlets
