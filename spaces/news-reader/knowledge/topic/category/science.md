## Science Category

Covers research breakthroughs, space exploration, climate science, physics, biology, medicine, and environmental issues.

### Classification signals

Keywords: research, study, discovery, experiment, journal, peer review, Nature, Science, PNAS, climate, environment, space, NASA, ESA, physics, chemistry, biology, genome, CRISPR, quantum, telescope, Mars

### Sub-topics

- Space exploration and astronomy
- Climate science and environment
- Physics and chemistry breakthroughs
- Biology and genetics
- Medical research (distinct from health policy)
- Environmental policy and conservation
- Scientific methodology and peer review
- Science policy and funding

### Trusted sources for science

Nature, Science, PNAS (primary journals), Scientific American, New Scientist, Quanta Magazine (mathematics/physics), EurekAlert (press releases — verify claims).

### Classification notes

- Distinguish between published, peer-reviewed research and pre-prints
- Single-study claims need the caveat "more research needed"
- Press releases about studies often exaggerate findings — check the actual paper
- Science reporting should include effect sizes and sample sizes, not just "significant" claims
