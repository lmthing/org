## Principle

Baking is **precision cooking**: small deviations in ratios, temperature, or timing produce measurably different results. Unlike stovetop cooking where you can taste-and-adjust continuously, baked goods are largely committed once they enter the oven.

## The key variables

### Leavening
| Agent | Mechanism | Best for |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Baking soda (NaHCO₃) | Reacts with acid instantly; single-acting | Recipes with buttermilk, yoghurt, honey, brown sugar |
| Baking powder | Contains acid + base; double-acting (once wet, once hot) | Neutral batters (all-purpose cakes, muffins) |
| Yeast | Biological; CO₂ + alcohol; slow | Bread, pizza, enriched doughs |
| Mechanical (air) | Whipping fat + sugar, or eggs | Creaming method, génoise, soufflé |
| Steam | Water vapour expansion | Choux, croissant, puff pastry |

### Fat role
- **Tenderness**: fat coats flour proteins, limiting gluten network (shortbread, tender cakes).
- **Flakiness**: solid fat in layers (pastry): laminate cold fat into dough; water in fat steams and separates layers on baking.
- **Moisture retention**: fat slows moisture loss; cakes with more fat stay moist longer.

### Flour protein content
| Type | Protein | Best for |
|------|---------|---------|
| Cake flour | 7–9% | Delicate, fine crumb cakes |
| All-purpose | 10–12% | General use |
| Bread flour | 12–14% | Chewy breads, pizza |
| Semolina / durum | 12–13% | Pasta, pizza doughs |

### Temperature precision
- **Oven calibration**: domestic ovens can be off by ±25°C. Use an oven thermometer.
- **Ingredient temperature**: butter for creaming should be 18–21°C (leaves a thumbprint, not greasy). Cold butter = poor aeration. Melted = flat.
- **Egg temperature**: room-temperature eggs incorporate better into batters.

## Bread fundamentals

1. **Autolyse**: mix flour + water only; rest 20–60 min. Gluten forms with zero kneading.
2. **Bulk fermentation**: first rise at room temperature (2–4 h) or cold retard (8–16 h in fridge). Cold retard develops more flavour.
3. **Shaping**: surface tension is key — a tight outer skin traps gas and supports the loaf. Tuck and drag on an unfloured surface.
4. **Final proof**: until dough springs back slowly when poked (not immediately = over-proofed; not at all = under-proofed).
5. **Scoring**: ½–1cm deep cuts release steam and guide oven spring.
6. **Steam in the first 15 min**: keeps the crust pliable for oven spring. Dutch oven method is simplest for home bakers.

## Pastry essentials

- **Keep fat cold**: for shortcrust and puff, fat must remain solid to create flaky layers. Work quickly; chill dough if it warms.
- **Don't overwork**: gluten develops with mixing. Overworked shortcrust = tough. Mix until just combined; rest before rolling.
- **Blind baking**: pre-bake an empty tart shell (line with parchment + weights) to prevent a soggy bottom for wet fillings.
- **Egg wash**: 1 yolk + 1 tbsp cream gives the deepest golden-brown colour. Whole egg + water = lighter, shinier.
