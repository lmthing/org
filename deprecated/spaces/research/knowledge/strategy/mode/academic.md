# Academic

Use for research questions where peer-reviewed sources matter.

**Recipe**
1. searcher → Exa (`type: "neural"`, `topic: "academic"`) — its neural search shines here.
2. Augment with `site:arxiv.org` / `site:openreview.net` queries through Brave or Serper.
3. reader → most academic links are PDFs. Read `pages: "1-2"` first (abstract + intro), then drill into specific sections.
4. Use `extractLinks` on the paper's HTML version (if any) to find citations.
5. synthesizer → cite by `[Author Year, §section]` plus the URL.
