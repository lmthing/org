## Topic Deep Dive Format

A detailed briefing focused entirely on one topic, drawing from multiple sources to provide depth, context, and analysis.

### Structure

```
# Deep Dive: <topic> — <date>

## Overview
<3-5 paragraph summary of the current state of this topic,
synthesised from multiple sources>

## Timeline
- <date>: <key event>
- <date>: <key event>
- ...

## Key Players
| Entity | Role | Recent Activity |
|--------|------|-----------------|
| ...    | ...  | ...             |

## Source Perspectives
### <source-1>
<framing summary + key claims>

### <source-2>
<framing summary + key claims>

## What We Know
- <confirmed claim with [source-N] citations>
- ...

## What's Unclear
- <open question or disputed claim>
- ...

## What to Watch
- <upcoming event or expected development>
- ...

## Sources
[source-1] <Title> — <url>
[source-2] ...
```

### Content rules

- Minimum 5 sources, maximum 10
- Every factual claim must be cited
- Distinguish confirmed facts from speculation
- Include a timeline of key events
- Identify the main actors and their positions
- Flag disagreements between sources explicitly
- End with forward-looking "What to Watch" section
