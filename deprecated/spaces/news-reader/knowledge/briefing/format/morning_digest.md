## Morning Digest Format

A comprehensive morning briefing that covers the top stories across all subscribed feeds, organised by topic category.

### Structure

```
# Morning Briefing — <date>

## Lead Story
<Featured article with full summary from the most important story>

## Politics (N stories)
- **Headline** — [source](url)
- ...

## Technology (N stories)
- **Headline** — [source](url)
- ...

## Business (N stories)
- ...

## Science (N stories)
- ...

## Health (N stories)
- ...

## World (N stories)
- ...

---
*Generated from N articles across M feeds.*
```

### Content rules

- Lead story gets a 2-3 paragraph summary
- Each category lists up to 5 top stories
- Featured article per category gets a 1-paragraph deep-dive
- All stories link to source URL
- Credibility scores shown for sources below 0.7
- Opinion pieces tagged with [Opinion]
- Breaking news tagged with [BREAKING]
