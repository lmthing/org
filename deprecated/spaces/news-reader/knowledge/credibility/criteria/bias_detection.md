## Bias Detection Framework

Media bias manifests in multiple dimensions. Use these techniques to identify and assess bias systematically.

### Types of bias

1. **Selection bias** — What stories the outlet chooses to cover or ignore
2. **Framing bias** — How a story is presented (word choice, context, emphasis)
3. **Source bias** — Which voices are amplified or excluded
4. **Placement bias** — Where stories appear (front page vs buried)
5. **Omission bias** — What relevant facts are left out

### Language indicators

**Loaded/emotional language:**
- Positive spin: "bold move", "sweeping reform", "triumph", "landmark"
- Negative spin: "slams", "blasts", "explodes", "crumbles", "disaster"
- Fear-based: "crisis", "threat", "danger", "alarming", "terrifying"
- Outrage-based: "outrageous", "scandalous", "shocking", "disgusting"

**Hedging language:**
- "Some say", "critics claim", "many believe" — often masks unsupported claims
- "Sources say" — anonymous sourcing without verification
- "Could", "might", "may" — speculative language presented as news

### Structural indicators

- **Headline-body mismatch**: Sensationalised headlines that the article body doesn't support
- **False equivalence**: Presenting fringe views as equivalent to scientific consensus
- **Cherry-picking**: Selecting only data points that support a narrative
- **Straw man**: Misrepresenting an opposing position to make it easier to attack
- **Appeal to authority**: Citing authority figures outside their expertise

### Cross-reference technique

When you suspect bias, verify by:

1. Search for the same story on outlets with different bias ratings
2. Compare which facts are included/excluded across sources
3. Compare framing — is the same event described with different emotional tone?
4. Check if quotes are presented in full context or selectively edited

### Bias rating scale

| Rating | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Left | Consistently favours progressive/left-wing perspectives |
| Left-center | Mild left-leaning tendency; generally factual |
| Center | Balanced coverage; minimal detectable bias |
| Right-center | Mild right-leaning tendency; generally factual |
| Right | Consistently favours conservative/right-wing perspectives |
| Unknown | Insufficient data to assess |
