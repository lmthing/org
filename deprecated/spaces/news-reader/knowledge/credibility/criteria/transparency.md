## Transparency Assessment

Transparency measures how open a source is about its operations, funding, and decision-making.

### Ownership transparency

- Is the ownership structure publicly disclosed?
- Is the parent company or holding entity identifiable?
- Are there known conflicts of interest between ownership and coverage areas?
- Is the outlet independent or state-controlled?

### Funding transparency

- Are revenue sources disclosed? (advertising, subscriptions, grants, donations, state funding)
- Is there a paywall, and how does it affect access?
- Are sponsored or paid-content sections clearly labelled?
- Is the outlet funded by entities it covers regularly?

### Methodological transparency

- Does the outlet explain its reporting methodology?
- Are polling and survey methods documented?
- Are data sources cited and verifiable?
- Is there a public editor or ombudsman?

### Author transparency

- Are author backgrounds and potential conflicts disclosed?
- Can readers contact authors directly?
- Do authors disclose financial relationships with subjects they cover?

### Rating scale

| Score | Behaviour |
|-------|-----------|
| 0.9–1.0 | Full disclosure: ownership, funding, methodology, and authorship are transparent |
| 0.7–0.9 | Most information available; minor gaps |
| 0.5–0.7 | Some transparency; significant gaps in ownership or funding disclosure |
| 0.3–0.5 | Limited transparency; ownership or funding obscured |
| 0.0–0.3 | Opaque: no transparency about operations |
