## Business Category

Covers markets, companies, economy, finance, trade, mergers, earnings, and industry trends.

### Classification signals

Keywords: market, stock, earnings, revenue, profit, merger, acquisition, IPO, trade, economy, GDP, inflation, interest rate, central bank, Fed, ECB, startup, unicorn, venture capital, private equity

### Sub-topics

- Stock markets and financial markets
- Company earnings and performance
- Mergers, acquisitions, and IPOs
- Economic indicators (GDP, inflation, employment)
- Central bank policy and interest rates
- International trade and tariffs
- Industry analysis and trends
- Personal finance and consumer economics

### Trusted sources for business

Bloomberg, Financial Times, Wall Street Journal, Reuters (financial wire), The Economist (analysis).

### Classification notes

- Market coverage is inherently time-sensitive — note publication dates
- Distinguish between market speculation and economic data
- Company earnings reports are primary sources — prefer direct reports over analyst interpretations
- Economic forecasts are probabilistic — always note uncertainty ranges
