## World Category

Covers international affairs, conflicts, humanitarian crises, development, and regional news outside the user's home country.

### Classification signals

Keywords: conflict, war, peace, treaty, refugee, humanitarian, crisis, summit, UN, NATO, EU, ASEAN, foreign, international, bilateral, multilateral, sanctions, aid, development, migration

### Sub-topics

- Armed conflicts and wars
- Diplomatic relations and summits
- Humanitarian crises and refugees
- International organisations (UN, EU, NATO, etc.)
- Regional political developments
- Trade agreements and economic partnerships
- Environmental and climate agreements
- Migration and displacement

### Trusted sources for world news

Reuters, AP (wire services), BBC, Al Jazeera (Middle East perspective), DW (European), France24, NHK (Japan).

### Classification notes

- International coverage often reflects the home country's perspective — cross-reference across regions
- Conflict reporting is particularly susceptible to propaganda — verify through multiple independent sources
- Be cautious with casualty figures in conflict zones — numbers are often contested
- Distinguish between government statements and verified facts
