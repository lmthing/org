## Health Category

Covers public health, disease outbreaks, medical treatments, healthcare policy, mental health, and nutrition.

### Classification signals

Keywords: health, disease, outbreak, pandemic, vaccine, treatment, clinical trial, WHO, CDC, hospital, medical, drug, therapy, mental health, nutrition, diet, exercise, wellness, epidemic, virus, bacteria

### Sub-topics

- Disease outbreaks and pandemics
- Vaccine development and distribution
- Clinical trials and drug approvals
- Healthcare policy and insurance
- Mental health and psychology
- Nutrition and diet science
- Public health guidelines
- Hospital and healthcare system news

### Trusted sources for health

WHO, CDC, NIH (primary), Lancet, NEJM, BMJ (journals), STAT News, Reuters Health, Kaiser Health News.

### Classification notes

- Health reporting is high-stakes — inaccurate information can cause harm
- Always verify health claims against primary sources (published studies, official guidelines)
- Distinguish between preliminary research and established medical consensus
- Be cautious with "breakthrough" claims — most early-stage research does not lead to treatments
- Nutrition science is particularly noisy — single-food studies are unreliable
