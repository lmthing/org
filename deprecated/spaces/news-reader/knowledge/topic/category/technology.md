## Technology Category

Covers software, hardware, AI, startups, cybersecurity, privacy, tech policy, and digital culture.

### Classification signals

Keywords: AI, artificial intelligence, machine learning, software, hardware, startup, venture capital, cybersecurity, privacy, data breach, app, platform, cloud, semiconductor, chip, API, open source, blockchain, crypto, smartphone, computer, internet

### Sub-topics

- Artificial intelligence and machine learning
- Cybersecurity and data breaches
- Tech policy and regulation
- Startups and venture capital
- Product launches and reviews
- Internet culture and social media
- Semiconductors and hardware
- Privacy and surveillance

### Trusted sources for technology

Ars Technica, The Verge, TechCrunch (startup/VC focus), Wired, MIT Technology Review, IEEE Spectrum for deep technical coverage.

### Classification notes

- Product announcements often blur into marketing — distinguish reporting from PR
- AI coverage is particularly prone to hype — verify claims against published research
- Security stories require technical accuracy — prefer outlets with technical expertise
