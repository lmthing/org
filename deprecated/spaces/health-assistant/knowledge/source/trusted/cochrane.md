# Cochrane Library

- **Main URL:** https://www.cochranelibrary.com/
- **Cochrane Reviews:** https://www.cochranelibrary.com/cdsr/reviews
- **Cochrane Clinical Answers:** https://www.cochranelibrary.com/cca
- **Plain Language Summaries:** Available on every review page

## What It Excels At

- Gold-standard systematic reviews and meta-analyses of healthcare interventions
- Evidence-based answers to specific clinical questions
- Plain language summaries making complex reviews accessible to patients
- GRADE-certified certainty of evidence assessments
- Methodological rigor in minimizing bias in evidence synthesis

## Search Tips

- Start with **Cochrane Clinical Answers (CCA)** for quick, actionable answers to clinical questions
- Use the advanced search with MeSH terms, intervention comparisons, and population filters
- Each review has a standardized structure: background, methods, results, discussion, authors' conclusions
- Read the **"Plain Language Summary"** first for a non-technical overview before diving into the full review
- Use the **"Summary of Findings"** table for a quick view of effect sizes and certainty of evidence
- Filter by review status (new, updated, withdrawn) and topic (Cochrane Review Groups)
- Check the review's **"Characteristics of Included Studies"** table for study details and risk of bias ratings

## Authority Level

**Tier 1 -- Highest.** Cochrane is an independent, non-profit organization recognized as the international gold standard for evidence-based healthcare systematic reviews. Reviews follow a published protocol and are peer-reviewed. Widely cited in clinical practice guidelines worldwide.
