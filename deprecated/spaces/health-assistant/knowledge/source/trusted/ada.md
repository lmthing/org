# American Diabetes Association (ADA)

- **Main URL:** https://www.diabetes.org/
- **Standards of Care:** https://professional.diabetes.org/clinical-resources/standards-care
- **Professional Resources:** https://professional.diabetes.org/
- **Diabetes Care Journal:** https://diabetesjournals.org/care

## What It Excels At

- Comprehensive annual Standards of Medical Care in Diabetes guidelines
- Glycemic management targets and treatment algorithms (Type 1 and Type 2)
- Diabetic complication screening and management (retinopathy, nephropathy, neuropathy)
- Nutrition therapy and medical nutrition therapy recommendations
- Diabetes technology guidance (CGM, insulin pumps, automated insulin delivery)

## Search Tips

- The **Standards of Care** are updated annually and published as a supplement in *Diabetes Care* each January
- Use the **"Standards of Care App"** or web-based interactive version for point-of-care reference
- Recommendations are graded using the **ADA Evidence Grading System** (A = clear evidence from RCTs, B = supportive evidence, C = supportive evidence from poorly controlled studies, E = expert consensus)
- Search the **Diabetes Care** and **Clinical Diabetes** journals for primary research
- The **patient-facing** diabetes.org site provides food and fitness resources and risk tests
- Check the **"Standards of Care in Diabetes"** abridged version for primary care providers

## Authority Level

**Tier 1 -- Highest.** The ADA Standards of Care represent the most widely followed clinical practice guidelines for diabetes management in the United States and are recognized internationally. Updated annually by an expert committee, the guidelines synthesize the latest evidence and are endorsed by multiple professional organizations.
