# Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)

- **Main URL:** https://www.cdc.gov/
- **A-Z Index:** https://www.cdc.gov/az/
- **Data & Statistics:** https://www.cdc.gov/data-statistics/
- **Travel Health:** https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel

## What It Excels At

- Infectious disease surveillance, outbreak data, and epidemiological statistics
- Immunization and vaccine schedules for all age groups
- Travel health notices and destination-specific recommendations
- Workplace safety and environmental health guidelines
- Chronic disease prevalence and behavioral risk factor data (via BRFFS)

## Search Tips

- Use the **A-Z Index** for quick navigation to specific diseases or topics
- Search within a specific CDC Center (e.g., NCHHSTP, NCIRD) for focused results
- Check the **"Data & Statistics"** section for interactive dashboards, WONDER databases, and downloadable datasets
- Use **CDC WONDER** (https://wonder.cdc.gov/) for custom queries on mortality, natality, and communicable disease data
- Look for **"Clinical Guidance"** pages for healthcare provider-specific recommendations
- Travel health pages include recommended vaccines, malaria prophylaxis, and health alerts by destination

## Authority Level

**Tier 1 -- Highest.** The CDC is the U.S. federal agency under the Department of Health and Human Services charged with protecting public health and safety. Content is authored by epidemiologists, physicians, and public health experts. The definitive source for infectious disease guidance, vaccine schedules, and population-level health data in the United States.
