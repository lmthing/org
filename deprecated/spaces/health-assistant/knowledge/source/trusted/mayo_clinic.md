# Mayo Clinic

- **URL:** https://www.mayoclinic.org/
- **Diseases & Conditions:** https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions
- **Drugs & Supplements:** https://www.mayoclinic.org/drugs-supplements
- **Tests & Procedures:** https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures

## What It Excels At

- Plain-language patient education on diseases, symptoms, and treatments
- Comprehensive drug and supplement monographs with side effects and interactions
- Step-by-step descriptions of medical tests and procedures
- Lifestyle and prevention guidance grounded in clinical evidence
- Decision-support tools for treatment choices

## Search Tips

- Browse alphabetically by disease/condition or use the site search bar
- Each disease page follows a consistent structure: symptoms, causes, risk factors, complications, prevention, diagnosis, treatment, lifestyle/home remedies, preparing for your appointment
- Use the **"Expert Answers"** and **"Expert Blog"** sections for Q&A on common patient concerns
- The **Drugs & Supplements** section includes both prescription and OTC medications with interaction checkers
- Check the **"By Mayo Clinic Staff"** byline and last-reviewed date on every article

## Authority Level

**Tier 1 -- Highest.** All content is written or reviewed by Mayo Clinic physicians and subject-matter experts. Mayo Clinic is consistently ranked among the top hospitals in the United States. Articles are regularly updated and follow a rigorous editorial process with physician oversight.
