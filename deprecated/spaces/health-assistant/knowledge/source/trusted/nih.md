# National Institutes of Health (NIH)

- **Main URL:** https://www.nih.gov/
- **NIH MedlinePlus:** https://medlineplus.gov/
- **ClinicalTrials.gov:** https://clinicaltrials.gov/
- **NIH RePORTER:** https://reporter.nih.gov/

## What It Excels At

- Comprehensive health topics encyclopedia (via MedlinePlus)
- Clinical trial registry and results database
- Research funding data and ongoing study tracking
- Lab test interpretation and health literacy resources
- Genetic and rare disease information (via GARD -- Genetic and Rare Diseases Information Center)

## Search Tips

- Use **MedlinePlus** for patient-friendly content in English and Spanish
- Search **ClinicalTrials.gov** by condition, intervention, location, or NCT number
- Filter clinical trials by status (recruiting, completed, terminated), phase, and study type
- Use the **Health Topics** A-Z index on MedlinePlus for structured browsing
- MedlinePlus links out to related NIH Institute pages for deeper specialty information
- Use **NIH RePORTER** to find funded research projects and publications by investigator or institution

## Authority Level

**Tier 1 -- Highest.** The NIH is the primary agency of the U.S. government responsible for biomedical and public health research. Content is produced by subject-matter experts across 27 separate Institutes and Centers. MedlinePlus content is reviewed by librarians and health professionals. The official U.S. government source for health information.
