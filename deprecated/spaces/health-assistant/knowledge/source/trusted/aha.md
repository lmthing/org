# American Heart Association (AHA)

- **Main URL:** https://www.heart.org/
- **Professional Guidelines:** https://professional.heart.org/en/guidelines
- **AHA Journals:** https://www.ahajournals.org/
- **Heart Disease & Stroke Statistics:** Published annually in *Circulation*

## What It Excels At

- Cardiovascular disease prevention, diagnosis, and treatment guidelines
- Cardiac arrest and resuscitation protocols (ACLS, BLS, PALS)
- Stroke assessment and management (NINDS/AHA guidelines)
- Heart failure classification and management (ACC/AHA staging)
- Lipid management and hypertension treatment targets

## Search Tips

- Start with **Professional Guidelines** for condition-specific clinical practice guidelines co-issued with ACC
- Use the **AHA Journals** search (Circulation, Stroke, Hypertension, JACC) for primary research
- Look for **Scientific Statements** which synthesize evidence on specific topics without formal guideline recommendations
- Check the **"Classes of Recommendation"** (I, IIa, IIb, III) and **"Levels of Evidence"** (A, B-R, B-NR, C-LD, C-EO) on each guideline
- The **patient-facing** heart.org site has risk calculators (ASCVD risk, BMI) and lifestyle guidance
- Use the **"Guidelines-at-a-Glance"** interactive tools for quick point-of-care reference

## Authority Level

**Tier 1 -- Highest.** The AHA is the leading U.S. organization for cardiovascular science and education. Guidelines are co-developed with the American College of Cardiology (ACC) using rigorous systematic review methodology. AHA guidelines are the standard of care for cardiovascular disease management in the United States.
