## Common Tests and Procedures

- **X-Ray (AP/Lateral/Oblique)** -- first-line for fractures, arthritis, alignment
- **MRI** -- soft tissue injury (ligaments, tendons, menisci, cartilage, spinal cord)
- **CT Scan** -- complex fracture characterization, surgical planning
- **DEXA Scan** -- bone density measurement (T-score for osteoporosis)
- **Arthrocentesis (joint aspiration)** -- synovial fluid analysis (crystals, WBC, culture)
- **Arthroscopy** -- minimally invasive joint visualization and repair
- **EMG/NCS** -- radiculopathy vs. peripheral neuropathy differentiation
- **Bone Scan** -- metastatic disease, stress fractures, infection
- **ACL Reconstruction** -- tendon graft ACL replacement
- **Joint Injection (corticosteroid / viscosupplementation)** -- therapeutic and diagnostic

## Red-Flag Symptoms

- **Limb deformity + inability to bear weight + crepitus** -- fracture (immobilize, neurovascular check, X-ray)
- **5 P's: Pain out of proportion + Pallor + Paresthesia + Pulselessness + Paralysis** -- compartment syndrome (emergent fasciotomy)
- **Hip pain + inability to walk after fall in elderly** -- femoral neck fracture (orthopedic emergency, high mortality)
- **Severe back pain + fever + neurological deficit** -- epidural abscess (emergent MRI, antibiotics, surgery)
- **Hot swollen joint + fever** -- septic arthritis until proven (emergent arthrocentesis, do not delay)
- **Posterior knee dislocation** -- popliteal artery injury (CT angiogram mandatory, check distal pulses)
- **Cauda equina syndrome** -- saddle anesthesia, urinary retention, bilateral leg weakness (emergent MRI, surgical decompression)
- **Open fracture** -- surgical emergency (antibiotics + tetanus + irrigation and debridement within 6 hours)
- **Pathological fracture with minimal trauma** -- metastatic bone disease or metabolic bone disorder (oncology workup)
- **Hip pain in child with limp + limited internal rotation** -- slipped capital femoral epiphysis (SCFE) or Legg-Calve-Perthes
