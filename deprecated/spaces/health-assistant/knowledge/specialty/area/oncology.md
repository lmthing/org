# Oncology

## Key Terms and Abbreviations

| Term | Abbreviation | Meaning |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Tumor-Node-Metastasis | TNM | Cancer staging system |
| Overall Survival | OS | Time from diagnosis/treatment to death |
| Progression-Free Survival | PFS | Time to disease progression or death |
| Disease-Free Survival | DFS | Time without recurrence after curative treatment |
| Objective Response Rate | ORR | CR + PR on imaging |
| Complete Response | CR | No detectable disease |
| Partial Response | PR | >= 30% decrease in tumor burden |
| Progressive Disease | PD | >= 20% increase or new lesions |
| Performance Status | ECOG / KPS | Functional status scoring |
| Neutropenic Fever | FN | Fever + ANC < 500, oncologic emergency |
| Tumor Lysis Syndrome | TLS | Metabolic emergency from rapid cell death |
| Immune-Checkpoint Inhibitor | ICI | Anti-PD-1/PD-L1/CTLA-4 therapy |
| Chimeric Antigen Receptor | CAR-T | Engineered T-cell immunotherapy |
| Sentinel Lymph Node Biopsy | SLNB | Staging lymph node procedure |
| Adjuvant Therapy | -- | Post-surgery treatment to prevent recurrence |
| Neoadjuvant Therapy | -- | Pre-surgery treatment to shrink tumor |

## Common Tests and Procedures

- **CT Chest/Abdomen/Pelvis** -- staging, response assessment
- **PET-CT (FDG)** -- metabolic activity, staging, restaging
- **MRI (with contrast)** -- brain metastases, liver, bone, soft tissue
- **Bone Scan** -- skeletal metastases screening
- **Biopsy (core needle / excisional)** -- tissue diagnosis and molecular profiling
- **Molecular / Genomic Testing (NGS panel)** -- actionable mutations (EGFR, ALK, KRAS, BRAF, HER2, MSI, TMB)
- **Liquid Biopsy (ctDNA)** -- minimal residual disease, treatment monitoring
- **Sentinel Lymph Node Biopsy** -- axillary, inguinal staging
- **Bone Marrow Biopsy** -- hematologic malignancy staging
- **Tumor Markers** -- monitoring response and recurrence (not screening)

## Red-Flag Symptoms

- **New unexplained mass** growing over weeks -- biopsy indicated, do not watch and wait
- **Unexplained weight loss > 10% body weight** -- malignancy workup (age-appropriate cancer screening + CT)
- **New onset jaundice with palpable gallbladder** (Courvoisier sign) -- pancreatic head cancer
- **Unilateral leg swelling + risk factors** -- DVT (may be first presentation of occult cancer)
- **Hypercalcemia in cancer patient** -- humoral hypercalcemia of malignancy (PTHrP) or bone metastases
- **Acute onset severe back pain + leg weakness** -- spinal cord compression (emergent MRI, dexamethasone, radiation)
- **Fever + ANC < 500** -- neutropenic fever (draw cultures, start empiric antibiotics within 1 hour)
- **Tumor lysis syndrome** (rising K+, PO4, uric acid, falling Ca2+) -- aggressive IV hydration, rasburicase/allopurinol
- **Superior vena cava syndrome** -- facial/upper extremity edema, dyspnea, JVD (emergent radiation or stent)
- **New neurological deficits in cancer patient** -- brain metastases, leptomeningeal disease, or paraneoplastic syndrome
