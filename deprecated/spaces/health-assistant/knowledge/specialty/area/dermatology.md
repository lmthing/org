# Dermatology

|------|-------------|---------|

## Common Tests and Procedures

- **Skin Biopsy (punch, shave, excisional)** -- histopathologic diagnosis
- **Dermoscopy** -- pigmented lesion evaluation (ABCDE, pattern analysis)
- **KOH Preparation** -- fungal infection confirmation (tinea, candida)
- **Skin Scraping (scabies prep)** -- mite identification under microscope
- **Patch Testing** -- allergic contact dermatitis identification
- **Tzanck Smear** -- HSV/VZV multinucleated giant cells
- **Direct Immunofluorescence (DIF)** -- autoimmune blistering diseases (pemphigus, bullous pemphigoid)
- **Wood Lamp** -- vitiligo, tinea capitis, erythrasma
- **Mohs Surgery** -- BCC/SCC with margin control
- **Cryotherapy (liquid nitrogen)** -- warts, actinic keratoses, superficial skin cancers
- **Phototherapy (NB-UVB, PUVA)** -- psoriasis, eczema, vitiligo

## Red-Flag Symptoms

- **Widespread skin pain + blistering + mucosal involvement** -- SJS/TEN (stop offending drug immediately, burn center referral, do NOT treat as simple rash)
- **Rapidly expanding dark lesion with irregular borders** -- melanoma (ABCDE criteria, urgent excisional biopsy with narrow margins)
- **Purpura that does not blanch** -- vasculitis or coagulopathy (skin biopsy for HSP, ITP, TTP evaluation)
- **Extremely painful hemorrhagic crusts + mucosal erosions** -- pemphigus vulgaris (urgent biopsy + DIF, systemic corticosteroids)
- **Fever + diffuse erythema + desquamation** -- toxic shock syndrome or staphylococcal scalded skin syndrome
- **Non-healing ulcer in sun-exposed area** -- SCC or BCC (biopsy, consider immunosuppression risk)
- **Targetoid lesions with mucosal involvement** -- SJS (not simple erythema multiforme if mucosal)
- **Sudden onset angioedema + respiratory distress** -- anaphylaxis or hereditary angioedema (epinephrine if anaphylaxis)
- **Suspected necrotizing fasciitis** -- pain out of proportion, rapid spread, crepitus, gray discharge (surgical emergency, do not wait for imaging)
- **New-onset generalized rash + systemic symptoms + recent medication change** -- DRESS syndrome (drug reaction with eosinophilia and systemic symptoms)
