# Gastroenterology

## Common Tests and Procedures

- **Esophagogastroduodenoscopy (EGD)** -- upper GI visualization, biopsy, banding, dilation
- **Colonoscopy** -- colorectal cancer screening, polypectomy, IBD assessment
- **Endoscopic Ultrasound (EUS)** -- pancreatic masses, submucosal lesions, FNA
- **ERCP** -- biliary obstruction, stone extraction, stent placement
- **Capsule Endoscopy** -- obscure GI bleed, small bowel Crohn
- **MRCP** -- non-invasive biliary/pancreatic duct imaging
- **CT Abdomen/Pelvis (with contrast)** -- bowel wall thickening, abscess, masses
- **Liver Biopsy** -- fibrosis staging, steatohepatitis, autoimmune hepatitis
- **FibroScan (Transient Elastography)** -- non-invasive liver stiffness/fibrosis assessment
- **Fecal Calprotectin** -- intestinal inflammation marker (IBD vs. IBS)
- **Fecal Immunochemical Test (FIT)** -- colorectal cancer screening
- **H. pylori testing** (urea breath, stool antigen, serology) -- peptic ulcer disease

## Red-Flag Symptoms

- **Hematemesis or coffee-ground emesis** -- upper GI bleed (risk-stratify with Glasgow-Blatchford or Rockall score)
- **Melena (black tarry stools)** -- upper GI bleed until proven (hemoglobin > 1.5 g/dL drop typical)
- **Hematochezia with hemodynamic instability** -- can be massive upper GI bleed
- **Severe acute epigastric pain radiating to back** -- acute pancreatitis (check lipase, Ranson/UHIDS criteria)
- **Acute onset severe abdominal pain + rigid abdomen** -- perforated viscus (upright CXR for free air)
- **Jaundice + fever + RUQ pain** (Charcot triad) -- ascending cholangitis (urgent ERCP)
- **Progressive dysphagia to solids** -- esophageal cancer, stricture, achalasia (EGD + manometry)
- **New-onset ascites** -- check SAAG (serum-ascites albumin gradient > 1.1 = portal hypertension)
- **Altered mental status + asterixis in cirrhotic** -- hepatic encephalopathy (lactulose + rifaximin)
- **Unexplained iron deficiency anemia in male or postmenopausal female** -- occult GI malignancy workup (colonoscopy + EGD)
