# Neurology

## Key Terms and Abbreviations

| Term | Abbreviation | Meaning |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Cerebrovascular Accident | CVA / Stroke | Ischemic or hemorrhagic brain injury |
| Transient Ischemic Attack | TIA | Stroke symptoms resolving < 24 hours |
| Modified Rankin Scale | mRS | Stroke outcome disability measure |
| National Institutes of Health Stroke Scale | NIHSS | Stroke severity scoring |
| Multiple Sclerosis | MS | Demyelinating CNS disease |
| Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis | ALS | Motor neuron disease |
| Guillain-Barre Syndrome | GBS | Acute inflammatory demyelinating polyneuropathy |
| Myasthenia Gravis | MG | Autoimmune neuromuscular junction disorder |
| Intracerebral Hemorrhage | ICH | Bleeding within brain parenchyma |
| Subarachnoid Hemorrhage | SAH | Bleeding in subarachnoid space |
| Status Epilepticus | SE | Seizure > 5 min or recurrent without recovery |
| Lumbar Puncture | LP | Cerebrospinal fluid sampling |
| Electroencephalogram | EEG | Brain electrical activity recording |
| Nerve Conduction Study / EMG | NCS/EMG | Peripheral nerve and muscle assessment |
| Mini-Mental State Examination | MMSE | Cognitive screening tool |
| Montreal Cognitive Assessment | MoCA | More sensitive cognitive screen |

## Common Tests and Procedures

- **Non-contrast Head CT** -- acute hemorrhage detection (first-line in ER)
- **CT Angiography (Head/Neck)** -- large vessel occlusion for thrombectomy decision
- **MRI Brain (with diffusion)** -- acute ischemia detection, demyelination, tumors
- **MRA (Head/Neck)** -- non-invasive vascular imaging
- **Digital Subtraction Angiography (DSA)** -- gold standard vascular imaging
- **Lumbar Puncture (LP)** -- CSF analysis (cells, protein, glucose, culture, oligoclonal bands)
- **EEG** -- seizure classification, encephalopathy, status epilepticus monitoring
- **EMG/NCS** -- neuropathy, radiculopathy, myopathy, NMJ disorders
- **Evoked Potentials (VEP, BAEP, SSEP)** -- demyelination pathway assessment
- **Carotid Ultrasound** -- carotid stenosis screening
- **Transcranial Doppler (TCD)** -- vasospasm monitoring (SAH), sickle cell stroke screening

## Red-Flag Symptoms

- **Sudden worst headache of life** ("thunderclap") -- SAH until ruled out (CT + LP if CT negative)
- **Acute facial or limb weakness + speech difficulty** -- stroke, note onset time for tPA/thrombectomy window
- **Seizure lasting > 5 minutes** -- status epilepticus, ABCs then benzodiazepine
- **Fever + neck stiffness + altered mental status** -- bacterial meningitis (do not delay antibiotics for LP)
- **Progressive ascending weakness + areflexia** -- Guillain-Barre (monitor FVC, intubate if < 20 mL/kg)
- **Acute severe back pain + saddle anesthesia + urinary retention** -- cauda equina syndrome (emergent MRI)
- **Unilateral vision loss + jaw claudication + headache in > 50 yr** -- giant cell (temporal) arteritis (start steroids immediately)
- **Worsening headache + papilledema** -- intracranial hypertension (imaging first to rule out mass)
- **New onset chorea in a child after strep infection** -- Sydenham chorea (rheumatic fever)
- **Progressive proximal muscle weakness + elevated CK** -- inflammatory myopathy (polymyositis/dermatomyositis, malignancy association)
