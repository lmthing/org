# Nephrology

## Key Terms and Abbreviations

| Term | Abbreviation | Meaning |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Acute Kidney Injury | AKI | Rapid decline in renal function (hours-days) |
| Chronic Kidney Disease | CKD | Progressive decline > 3 months |
| Estimated GFR | eGFR | Filtration rate estimate from creatinine |
| End-Stage Renal Disease | ESRD | eGFR < 15, requires renal replacement |
| Glomerulonephritis | GN | Glomerular inflammation |
| Nephrotic Syndrome | -- | Proteinuria > 3.5 g/day, edema, hypoalbuminemia |
| Hemodialysis | HD | Extracorporeal blood filtration |
| Peritoneal Dialysis | PD | Abdominal membrane dialysis |
| Continuous Renal Replacement Therapy | CRRT | Slow continuous dialysis for ICU |
| Renin-Angiotensin-Aldosterone System | RAAS | Blood pressure and volume regulation |
| Urine Albumin-to-Creatinine Ratio | UACR | Spot urine protein quantification |
| Fractional Excretion of Sodium | FENa | Distinguishes prerenal vs. intrinsic AKI |
| Anion Gap | AG | Metabolic acidosis classification |
| Transtubular Potassium Gradient | TTKG | Hyper/hypokalemia workup |

## Common Tests and Procedures

- **Basic Metabolic Panel (BMP)** -- creatinine, BUN, electrolytes, eGFR
- **Urinalysis with microscopy** -- hematuria, proteinuria, casts, crystals
- **Urine Albumin-to-Creatinine Ratio (UACR)** -- quantitative proteinuria
- **24-hour urine collection** -- creatinine clearance, protein quantification, electrolytes
- **Renal Ultrasound** -- kidney size, obstruction, cysts, echogenicity
- **Renal Biopsy** -- glomerular disease classification, transplant rejection
- **FENa / FEUrea** -- prerenal vs. ATN differentiation in AKI
- **Serum and Urine Protein Electrophoresis (SPEP/UPEP)** -- myeloma kidney
- **ANCA, Anti-GBM, Complement (C3/C4)** -- autoimmune glomerulonephritis workup
- **CT Urogram** -- nephrolithiasis, urothelial malignancy

## Red-Flag Symptoms

- **Oliguria/anuria** (< 400 mL/day) with rising creatinine -- AKI, obstruction must be ruled out
- **New-onset massive proteinuria** (> 3.5 g/day) with edema -- nephrotic syndrome workup (rule out malignancy in elderly)
- **Gross hematuria with dysmorphic RBCs/RBC casts** -- glomerulonephritis
- **Hyperkalemia > 6.5 mEq/L** with ECG changes -- emergency (peaked T waves, wide QRS)
- **Rapidly rising creatinine** (doubling in < 48 hr) -- think RPGN, requires urgent renal biopsy
- **Severe metabolic acidosis** (pH < 7.2, bicarb < 10) with high anion gap -- methanol, ethylene glycol, DKA, lactic acidosis
- **Hyponatremia < 120 mEq/L** or rapid drop -- seizure risk, requires careful correction
- **Flank pain + hematuria + abdominal bruit** -- renal artery stenosis / infarction
- **Fever + flank pain + bacteriuria in elderly** -- pyelonephritis with sepsis risk
- **Post-renal transplant: fever + rising creatinine + graft tenderness** -- acute rejection vs. infection
