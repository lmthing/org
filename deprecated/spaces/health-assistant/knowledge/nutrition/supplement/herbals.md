## Indications

Common herbal supplements and their primary uses:

| St. John's Wort | Mild-moderate depression | Moderate |
| Echinacea | Cold/URI prevention | Low-Moderate |

## Dosing Ranges

Standard adult dosing for well-studied herbs:

- **St. John's Wort**: 300 mg standardized extract (0.3% hypericin), 3x daily
- **Gingko Biloba**: 120-240 mg standardized extract (24% flavone glycosides), divided doses
- **Turmeric/Curcumin**: 500-2000 mg curcumin with piperine for absorption
- **Valerian root**: 300-600 mg, 30 min before bed
- **Saw Palmetto**: 320 mg daily

## Drug Interactions (Critical)

**St. John's Wort** is a potent CYP3A4 and P-glycoprotein inducer:
- Reduces effectiveness of: oral contraceptives, warfarin, cyclosporine, statins, benzodiazepines, HIV protease inhibitors
- Risk: serotonin syndrome when combined with SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs, triptans

**Gingko Biloba**: antiplatelet effects — increased bleeding risk with warfarin, aspirin, NSAIDs, clopidogrel

**Garlic extract**: antiplatelet and anticoagulant potentiation — increased bleeding risk

**Ginseng**: may interact with warfarin (reduced INR), stimulant effects with caffeine

## Deficiency Signs

Herbal supplements are not associated with deficiency states. Monitor for:
- Adverse effects specific to each herb
- Drug interaction signs (altered INR, serotonin syndrome, unexpected drug levels)
- Allergic reactions

## Lab Markers to Monitor

When patients use herbal supplements:
- **St. John's Wort**: liver enzymes (ALT/AST), serotonin levels (clinical), drug levels of concurrent medications
- **Gingko**: bleeding time, INR if on anticoagulants, platelet count
- **Turmeric**: liver enzymes at high doses, blood glucose (may lower)
- **Any herb**: CBC, CMP baseline and periodically

## Extraction Guidance

When extracting herbal supplement information from documents:
- Record the **specific product name** (brand matters — potency varies)
- Capture **standardization info** (e.g., "standardized to 24% flavone glycosides")
- Note **dosage and frequency**
- Flag **known drug interactions** with current medications
- Record **duration of use**
