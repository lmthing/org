## Symptom Patterns (Self-Care or Routine Appointment)

### General
- Common cold symptoms (runny nose, mild sore throat, low-grade fever <100.4°F/38°C, <7 days)
- Mild headache relieved by OTC medication
- Occasional mild heartburn relieved by antacids
- Minor muscle ache after activity (no swelling, deformity, or weakness)
- Mild fatigue without other symptoms
- Seasonal allergies with known triggers

### Musculoskeletal
- Mild muscle strain (improving after 2-3 days of rest)
- Minor bruise without significant swelling
- Mild joint stiffness in morning (improves with movement, <30 min)

### Dermatological
- Minor insect bite (no spreading redness, no difficulty breathing)
- Small superficial scrape or cut (not deep, not bleeding heavily)
- Mild sunburn (no blistering over large area)
- Mild acne flare

### Gastrointestinal
- Occasional mild nausea (no vomiting, resolves with rest)
- Mild occasional constipation (<3 days, no pain, no blood)
- Mild food sensitivity (bloating, gas) with identifiable trigger

### Mental Health
- Occasional stress or worry (manageable with self-care)
- Mild sleep difficulty (1-2 nights, improving)
- Mild mood fluctuation (situational, improving)

## Self-Care Recommendations

- Rest, hydration, OTC medication as directed
- Monitor symptoms — if they worsen, re-assess urgency
- Use telehealth for guidance if uncertain about severity
- Follow up with primary care at next available appointment for persistent symptoms

## Time-to-Treatment

- **Target: self-care with monitoring**
- Re-assess in 48-72 hours if symptoms persist
- Schedule routine appointment if symptoms last >1 week
- Telehealth visit optional for reassurance or guidance

## Care Setting

**Self-care at home** / **Telehealth** / **Routine primary care appointment**

## Red-Flag Escalation Criteria

Escalate to semi-urgent if symptoms:
- Persist >1 week without improvement
- Significantly affect daily activities
- Are accompanied by new concerning symptoms

Escalate to urgent if any red-flag symptoms develop (see [[semi_urgent]] or [[urgent]] criteria).
