# Yuki's research links

Real, publicly fetchable pages for the scenario's live `webSearch`/`webFetch` beat (Act II — "find me a
cheaper/closer clay supplier and tell me what the material actually is"). Each returns HTTP 200 to a
plain `curl -sIL -o /dev/null -w '%{http_code}'` (last checked 2026-07-13).

- Digitalfire — ceramic materials & clay-body reference — https://digitalfire.com/
  Why: the reference Yuki reaches for to check what a clay body / glaze material *is* before she swaps supplier.
- Valentine Clays — UK clay manufacturer (stoneware & porcelain bodies) — https://www.valentineclays.co.uk/
  Why: a real, live alternative-supplier candidate for her whiteware/stoneware clay — not Sibelco, not Ceramica IT.
- Glazy — open glaze & clay-body recipe database — https://glazy.org/
  Why: lets the research beat cross-check a glaze's materials against what she already stocks.
