# Links Nadia has open in tabs she never reads

Provenance for the research beat in this scenario. Her own files say *what she does* — rent on the
1st, chase at 14 days, deposits in the DPS — and never *what the law requires*, so the deposit
protection deadline and what happens when it is missed is a genuine knowledge miss: not in the
notes, not in the ledger, not in the tenancy list.

Stable, canonical, fetchable reference pages (no login, no paywall, no scraping defences):

- Tenancy deposit scheme (England and Wales) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenancy_deposit_scheme_(England_and_Wales)
  Why: the 30-day protection window and the prescribed information a landlord must serve — exactly
  the thing `offer-email.md` hands back to Nadia ("the scheme protection is yours to do"), and
  exactly the thing `portfolio-notes.md` calls "a three-times-the-deposit problem".
- Assured shorthold tenancy — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assured_shorthold_tenancy
  Why: the background an advising agent needs about terms, break clauses and notice before it says
  anything at all about a tenancy — and the reason a formal notice is never something to send on
  its own initiative.
- Landlord and Tenant Act 1985 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landlord_and_Tenant_Act_1985
  Why: the repairing obligations behind the "gas, smoke, electric, water coming in, no heat is an
  emergency" rule in `tickets-inbox.md`.

None of these facts appears in `portfolio-notes.md`, `tenancies.csv`, `ledger-export.csv`,
`tickets-inbox.md` or `offer-email.md` — checked, so a search here is a real miss and a *second*
search for the same thing is a real failure.
