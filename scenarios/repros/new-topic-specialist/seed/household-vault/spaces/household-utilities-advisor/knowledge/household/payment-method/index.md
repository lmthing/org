How household utility bills are paid and when they are due. The household pays electricity (PPC), water (EYDAP), and natural gas (Heron) bills from a single Piraeus bank account. The recorded bills show distinct due dates per utility, and the dashboard can use these to trigger payment reminders. No automated debit-order arrangements are recorded for the utilities — all known payments appear to be manual transfers from the main household account, so an explicit debit-order instruction does not exist yet for any of the three providers.
