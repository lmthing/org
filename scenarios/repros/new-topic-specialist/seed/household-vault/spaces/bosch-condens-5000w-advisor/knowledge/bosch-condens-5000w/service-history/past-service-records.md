# past-service-records

The real household documents (the warranty ledger and the household policies file) confirm the boiler purchase date and warranty expiry, but **do not contain dated service-event records or gas-meter readings**. The research summary references a last service on 2026-01-14 by Costas Xenakis (ThermoFix Akis) and a gas meter reading of 04821.6 m³ from January 2026; however, these specifics are not present in any attached document we can verify. What the actual documents do confirm is that the boiler is under an **annual legal service contract**. The Bosch installation and servicing manual (6 720 644 143, 2016/04) defines the full annual inspection protocol in Section 15 — including fault retrieval (service function 6.A), electrode check, heat-exchanger and burner inspection, condensate-trap cleaning, expansion-vessel pre-charge check, and gas-air-ratio verification — and Section 18 provides the commissioning-report template to be completed at each service visit.
