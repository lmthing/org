# electrical-requirements

Electrical supply and connection requirements from the Bosch Condens 5000 W manual (6 720 644 143, 2016/04), sections 3.10, 3.11, and 7.1.

## Power supply
- Voltage: **220 – 240 V AC**
- Frequency: **50 Hz**
- The appliance **must be earthed**.
- All pipes to the appliance must be earthed.
- No other electrical consumer units may be connected to the same power cable.

## Power consumption
- Max. power consumption (central heating mode): **150 W** (ZSB 30-2 A); **160 W** (ZWB 37-2 A)
- The ZWB 30-2 A falls within this range (~150–160 W).

## Fuses (manual section 7.1, Fig. 4/5)
- The appliance is protected by **three fuses** located on the circuit board:
  - Fuse slow T **0.5 A**
  - Fuse slow T **1.6 A**
  - Replacement fuses are stored on the back of the Heatronic cover.

## Safety & compliance
- All modulation, control, and safety components are tested and ready-wired.
- Observe safety measures according to AS/NZS 3000 (Electrical Installations).
- EMC limit class: **B**
- Appliance enclosure rating: **IP X4D** (splash-proof)

## Connection notes
- Only operate this appliance with a **Bosch controller** (e.g. FW 100 weather-compensated controller, FR 10/FR 110 room temperature controller, DT 10 RF/DT 20 RF digital time switch).
- A 2-wire BUS connection is used for controller communication.
- The connecting lead is supplied with a mains plug.
- When connecting accessories, always cut cable grips to fit cable diameter to maintain anti-splash protection (IP rating).
