# tech-data

Performance data for the Bosch Condens 5000 W, as documented in the installation and servicing manual (6 720 644 143, 2016/04).

The specific unit is a **ZWB 30-2 A** — a gas condensing combi boiler for central heating (CH) and domestic hot water (DHW) on the instantaneous water heating principle. The manual provides detailed specification tables for the ZSB 30-2 A (heating-only) and ZWB 37-2 A (combi) variants. The ZWB 30-2 A sits between them; its rated heating output aligns with the ZSB 30-2 A figures below.

## Heating output (natural gas, ZSB 30-2 A — closest documented variant)
- Max. heat output 80/60 °C: **30.0 kW**
- Max. heat output 50/30 °C: **31.8 kW**
- Max. heat output 40/30 °C: **32.1 kW**
- Min. heat output 80/60 °C: **7.7 kW**
- Max. DHW output (ZSB): **30.9 kW**
- Max. gas consumption (CH): **123.4 MJ/h**
- Gas supply rate (natural gas, gross heating value 37.8 MJ/m³): **3.2 m³/h**

## Permissible gas supply pressure
- Natural gas: **1.1 – 3.0 kPa**
- LPG (propane/butane): **2.75 kPa**

## Heating system
- Max. flow temperature: **approx. 90 °C**
- Max. permissible operating pressure (heating): **300 kPa**
- Expansion vessel: **10 L total capacity**, pre-charge pressure **75 kPa**
- Nominal capacity of appliance (heating): **3.5 L**
- Condensate: max. quantity **2.7 L/h** (tR = 30 °C), pH approx. **4.8**

## Noise & class
- Sound pressure level (heating mode): ≤ **43 dB(A)** (ZSB); ≤ **45 dB(A)** (ZWB 37-2)
- NOx class: **5**
- EMC limit class: **B**
- Appliance enclosure rating: **IP X4D**

## DHW (from ZWB 37-2 A spec, closest combi reference)
- Max. DHW flow rate: **13.0 L/min**
- Outlet temperature range: **40 – 60 °C**
- Max. cold water inlet temperature: **60 °C**
- Min. inlet pressure: **300 kPa**; max. permissible supply pressure: **1000 kPa**
- Specific flow rate (EN 625): **16.9 L/min**
