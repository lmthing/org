# inspection-checklist-section15

Section 15 of the Bosch Condens 5000 W service manual (6 720 644 143, 2016/04) defines the annual inspection and servicing procedure. The 16-point checklist (Section 15.1) is:

1. Retrieve last fault stored on the Heatronic module — service function 6.A.
2. Check filter in cold water pipe (ZWB models).
3. Perform a visual check of the air/flue gas routing.
4. Check the gas supply pressure.
5. Check the gas-air ratio (CO2 or O2) at minimum and maximum rated output; adjust if necessary.
6. Check for leaks on the gas and water connections.
7. Check the heat exchanger (clean if control pressure drops below 3.9 mbar / 0.39 kPa).
8. Check the burner (clean or replace components as needed).
9. Check electrodes (clean or replace, check tightness at 3+1 Nm).
10. Check the diaphragm in the mixer unit.
11. Clean the condensate trap (fill with approx. 1/4 litre water before refitting).
12. Check the expansion vessel pre-charge pressure matches the static head of the heating system.
13. Check the heating system pressure (optimum 1–2 bar when cold; max 3 bar at highest heating temperature before the pressure relief valve opens).
14. Check electrical wiring for physical damage.
15. Check the heating controller settings.
16. Check the selected service functions match the Heatronic settings label.

After servicing, the engineer must retighten all loosened threaded fittings, recommission the appliance, check all connections for leaks, and verify the gas-air ratio. Required test equipment includes an electronic flue gas emission meter for CO2/CO/exhaust temperature and a pressure gauge (0–30 mbar, minimum 0.1 mbar resolution). Only genuine spare parts should be used; all seals and O-rings removed during servicing must be renewed.
