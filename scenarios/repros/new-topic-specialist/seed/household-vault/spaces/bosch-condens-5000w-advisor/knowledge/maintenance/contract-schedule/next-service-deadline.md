# next-service-deadline

The last annual service was performed on 2026-01-14. The engineer set a hard deadline for the next service: the annual inspection must be completed by 2027-01-15.

The boiler must be serviced annually under the legal service contract, which replaced the expired manufacturer warranty (manufacturer warranty ended 2024-09-10). Per the Bosch service manual Section 15, an annual inspection is recommended as the minimum interval, and the service reminder function (5.A) in the Heatronic module tracks a burner service interval of 2324 hours of operation — when this elapses the display shows an alternating reminder.

Missing the 2027-01-15 deadline could leave the household without a valid service record, potentially voiding insurance or legal liability coverage related to the boiler. Dashboard reminders should alert the owner well in advance (e.g. 60 days and 30 days before the deadline).
