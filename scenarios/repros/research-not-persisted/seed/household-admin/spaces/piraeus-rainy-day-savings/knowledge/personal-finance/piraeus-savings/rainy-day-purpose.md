# rainy-day-purpose

## Designated Purpose

The policies.md document explicitly labels this Piraeus account as **"(rainy-day)"** —
a term that in personal finance denotes an emergency or contingency fund. This is the
only account in the household document given this designation.

## What It Covers

As a rainy-day fund, it is intended to absorb:
- **Income disruption** — temporary loss of Dimitris's income
- **Unexpected large expenses** — emergency home repairs, medical costs beyond insurance co-pay (€20/visit),
  or urgent car/scooter repairs not fully covered by insurance deductibles
- **Liquidity buffer** — ensuring mortgage payments (€1,042/mo) and essential bills continue uninterrupted

## What It Is Not

The document structure makes clear this is **not** the everyday spending account (that is the
Piraeus main current account), **not** the joint-expenses account (Revolut), and **not** a
retirement vehicle (that is the MetLife Silver private pension, €180/mo, policy MET-PEN-441).

## Size Relative to Needs

At €11,400, the fund covers roughly:
- ~10-11 months of the mortgage payment alone
- ~6-7 months of total core household outflows (mortgage + utilities + insurance + subscriptions)
This is a conventional emergency-fund sizing of approximately 6 months of expenses.
