# Component Checks

The Bosch Condens 5000 W (ZWB 37-2 A) servicing manual (6 720 644 143, 2016/04) details checks for each major component during the annual inspection:

### Heat exchanger
Measure the control pressure at the mixer unit test point at maximum rated output. The threshold for cleaning is < 3.9 mbar (0.39 kPa). A pressure at or above this value indicates the heat exchanger does not yet need cleaning. The condensate produced during operation has a pH of approximately 4.8 and a maximum quantity of 2.7 L/h at 30°C return temperature.

### Burner
Remove the burner cover after taking off the top casing (two screws) and releasing the clips and retaining nuts. Inspect the burner mesh and components for contamination, deformation, or corrosion. Always replace the gasket on reassembly and readjust the gas-air ratio afterwards. CO must be below 400 ppm dry and air-free at both maximum and minimum rated output.

### Electrodes
Both the ignition electrodes and the flame-sense electrode are part of a single electrode set. Remove the set and gasket; check for carbon deposits, erosion, or cracking of the ceramic insulators. Torque the securing screws to 3 ± 1 Nm on reassembly.

### Expansion vessel
The integrated vessel has a total capacity of 10 litres, pre-charged at the factory to 0.75 bar (75 kPa). If the system volume exceeds 100 litres or the pressure exceeds 2.65 bar (265 kPa) at maximum heating temperature, an additional expansion vessel must be fitted in the CH return. Both vessels must have the same pre-charge pressure.

### Condensate trap / siphon
Remove the trap and check that the heat exchanger connection is clear of debris. The condensate pipe must be a minimum of 40 mm Ø plastic pipe, falling at least 50 mm per metre towards the outlet. The siphon-fill programme (service function 4.F) ensures the trap is filled after long idle periods to prevent flue gas escape.

### Diaphragm in mixer unit
Open the mixing chamber and carefully withdraw the diaphragm from the fan intake tube. The diaphragm flaps must open upwards. Check for splits, hardening, or soiling. Replace if any damage is found.

### Plate-type heat exchanger (ZWB only)
If DHW output is insufficient and the cold water strainer is clean, the plate heat exchanger must be removed and either replaced with a new unit (using fresh gaskets) or descaled with a product approved for stainless steel (1.4401). Never use unapproved descaling agents.

### Cold water strainer (ZWB only)
Accessed by undoing the cold water pipe connection. A clogged strainer will reduce DHW flow and performance.

### Gas valve and gas-air ratio
Check the gas supply pressure at the test point during maximum rated output. Permissible ranges: NG 1.1–3.0 kPa, LPG (Propane) 2.5–4.5 kPa, LPG (Butane) 2.5–3.5 kPa. The gas-air ratio is checked via CO₂ or O₂ measurement at the flue gas test point (probe inserted approx. 135 mm). Adjust the gas flow throttle under the fan unit if values are outside ±0.5% CO₂ of spec; for post-cleaning or component replacement the tolerance tightens to ±0.2% CO₂.

Source: Bosch Condens 5000 W Installation and Servicing Instructions, document 6 720 644 143 (2016/04).
