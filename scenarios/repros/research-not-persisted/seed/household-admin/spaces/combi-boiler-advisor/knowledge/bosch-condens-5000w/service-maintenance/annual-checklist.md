# Annual Checklist

The manufacturer's inspection/servicing report (section 15.1 of the Bosch Condens 5000 W instructions, document 6 720 644 143) lists the following mandatory annual checks:

1. **Retrieve last fault** stored on the Heatronic module using service function 6.A.
2. **Check the filter in the cold water pipe** (ZWB combi models).
3. **Visual inspection of the air/flue gas routing** for integrity.
4. **Check the gas supply pressure** — permissible ranges: NG 1.1–3.0 kPa, LPG (Propane) 2.5–4.5 kPa, LPG (Butane) 2.5–3.5 kPa.
5. **Check the gas-air ratio** (CO₂ or O₂) at minimum and maximum rated output and adjust if necessary — NG max 9.6% CO₂ / min 9.0% CO₂, LPG Propane max 10.8% / min 10.2%, LPG Butane max 12.2% / min 11.5%.
6. **Leak test** all gas and water connections.
7. **Check the heat exchanger** — control pressure at maximum rated output; if below 3.9 mbar (0.39 kPa), cleaning is required.
8. **Check the burner** for wear and contamination.
9. **Check the electrodes** (ignition and flame sense) for dirt and correct gap.
10. **Check the diaphragm in the mixer unit** for soiling or splits.
11. **Clean the condensate trap** — remove, clean, refill with approximately 0.25 L of water.
12. **Check the expansion vessel pre-charge pressure** against the static head of the heating system (factory pre-charge 0.75 bar / 75 kPa).
13. **Check the heating system pressure** — cold minimum 1 bar (100 kPa), optimum 1–2 bar (100–200 kPa); the pressure relief valve opens at 3 bar (300 kPa).
14. **Check electrical wiring** for physical damage.
15. **Check the heating controller settings.**
16. **Verify service functions** against the 'Heatronic settings' label affixed to the casing.

The manufacturer recommends taking out an annual inspection/servicing contract with an authorised contractor to keep gas consumption and environmental impact as low as possible over time.
