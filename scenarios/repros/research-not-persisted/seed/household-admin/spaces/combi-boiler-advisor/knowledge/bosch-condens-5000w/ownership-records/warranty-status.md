# warranty-status

The Bosch Condens 5000 W gas combi boiler was purchased on 10 September 2022. The manufacturer's standard two-year warranty under EU rules expired on 10 September 2024. The boiler is now out of its original warranty period. Ongoing protection is provided through an annual legal service contract with a registered technician, which took effect after the warranty ended.
