Service and maintenance requirements for the Bosch Condens 5000 W (ZWB 37-2 A) gas condensing combi boiler. This field splits by aspect of the annual servicing cycle: what the manufacturer's inspection/servicing checklist covers, the detailed step-by-step procedures for each servicing operation, recommended cleaning intervals for critical components, and component-specific checks for the burner, heat exchanger, electrodes, expansion vessel, condensate trap, ignition electrodes, diaphragm in the mixer unit, strainer in the cold water pipe, and the plate-type heat exchanger. All content is sourced directly from the official Bosch installation and servicing instructions (document 6 720 644 143, 2016/04) and the household's own service records.
