# electrical-rating

The boiler requires a 220–240 V AC, 50 Hz single-phase supply and must be earthed. It draws a maximum of 160 W in central heating mode. The appliance enclosure is rated IP X4D, meaning it is protected against splashing water from any direction. The EMC limit class is B.

Three internal fuses protect the appliance: two slow-blow fuses rated T 0.5 A and T 1.6 A, plus a 24 V fuse (Si 3). The Heatronic 3 control module manages all modulation, safety, and control components, with a 2-wire BUS for connecting Bosch controllers such as the FW 100 weather-compensated unit. All pipes to the appliance must be earthed, and no other electrical consumer units may share the same power cable.
