# Inspection Procedures

Section 15.2 of the Bosch Condens 5000 W installation and servicing instructions (6 720 644 143, 2016/04) details each servicing operation. The procedures are:

### Retrieving last fault (service function 6.A)
Select service function 6.A via the Heatronic service menu to read the last stored fault code. This flags intermittent or recent issues before physical inspection begins.

### Checking electrodes
Remove the electrode set and gasket. Inspect both ignition and flame-sense electrodes for dirt or erosion; clean or replace as needed. Refit with a new gasket and torque to 3 ± 1 Nm (specified in the manual's torque figure).

### Strainer in cold water pipe (ZWB only)
Undo the cold water pipe connection and check the internal strainer for debris. Clean and refit.

### Plate-type heat exchanger (ZWB only)
If DHW output is insufficient, first check the cold water strainer. Then disconnect the electrical connectors, remove the pressure relief valve hose, undo the pipe unions, release the quick-acting closures on the hydraulic assembly, and remove the plate heat exchanger. Replace with a new unit and fresh gaskets, or descale using only a product approved for stainless steel (grade 1.4401).

### Heat exchanger
Measure control pressure at the mixer unit test point at maximum rated output. If below 3.9 mbar (0.39 kPa) for ZWB 37-2 A, cleaning is required. Remove the access panel, remove the condensate siphon (placing a container underneath), clean the heat exchanger from bottom to top with cleaning blade (accessory 1061), then from top to bottom with the brush (accessory 1060). Remove the burner and flush the heat exchanger with water from above. Clean the condensate tray and siphon connection. Reassemble with a new access panel gasket, torquing screws to approximately 5 Nm.

### Burner
Remove the top cover (two screws), release the clips, nuts, and two retaining screws, then lift off the burner cover. Remove the burner and clean all components. Refit with a new gasket in reverse order, then readjust the gas-air ratio.

### Diaphragm in mixer unit
Open the mixing chamber. Carefully withdraw the diaphragm from the fan intake tube and inspect for soiling and splits. The diaphragm flaps must open upwards on refitting. Close the mixing chamber.

### Condensate trap
Remove the condensate trap and check that the heat exchanger connection is clear. Remove and clean the condensate siphon sump. Check the condensate hose and clean if necessary. Fill the trap with approximately 0.25 L of water and refit.

### Expansion vessel
Depressurise the appliance and check the pre-charge pressure of the integrated 10 L expansion vessel (factory set to 0.75 bar / 75 kPa). Adjust to match the static head of the heating system if needed. Where system volume exceeds 100 litres or pressure exceeds 2.65 bar at maximum temperature, an additional expansion vessel must be fitted in the CH return.

### Heating system pressure
Check the pressure gauge when the system is cold. If below 1 bar (100 kPa), top up to 1–2 bar (100–200 kPa) using the filling loop. The maximum operating pressure is 3 bar (300 kPa); the pressure relief valve opens at that point.

### Electrical wiring
Visually inspect all electrical wiring for physical damage and replace any faulty leads.

Source: Bosch Condens 5000 W Installation and Servicing Instructions, document 6 720 644 143 (2016/04), section 15 — Inspection/Servicing.
