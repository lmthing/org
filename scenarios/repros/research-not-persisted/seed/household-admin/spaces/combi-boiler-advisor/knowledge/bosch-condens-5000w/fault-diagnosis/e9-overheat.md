# E9 — Overheat (Safety Temperature Limiter Tripped)

The E9 fault code means either the heat exchanger temperature limiter or the flue gas temperature limiter has tripped. The boiler locks out to prevent damage from excessive temperatures.

**From the manufacturer's service manual, the full remedy checklist is:**
- Check the heat exchanger temperature limiter and its lead for breaks or short circuits.
- Check the flue gas temperature limiter and its lead for breaks or short circuits.
- Verify the heating system operating pressure on the pressure gauge. If it is below 1 bar (100 kPa) when cold, top up to 1–2 bar (100–200 kPa).
- Check the pump — the pump starter or the pump itself may have failed, causing poor circulation and local overheating.
- Check the PCB fuse (fuses F1 and F2, slow-blow T 0.5 A and T 1.6 A, on the ZWB 37-2 wiring diagram).
- Vent the appliance thoroughly (service function 2.C activates the air purge mode).
- Inspect the heat exchanger on the water side for blockages or scale, and clean or replace if necessary.
- On appliances with displacement bodies inside the heat exchanger, confirm the displacement bodies are fitted correctly.

E9 is a safety lockout — the boiler will not restart until the cause is resolved and the limiter resets. If the fault recurs immediately after reset, do not keep resetting it; the underlying circulation or blockage issue must be found first.
