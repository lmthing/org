# EA — Flame Not Detected

The EA fault code means the Heatronic module attempted ignition but did not confirm a flame. This is one of the most common fault conditions on the Condens 5000 W.

**Possible causes (from the manufacturer's service manual):**
- Gas valve is closed or gas supply is interrupted.
- Gas supply pressure is outside the permissible range (NG: 1.1–3.0 kPa; LPG: 2.75 kPa).
- Ignition electrodes are dirty, misaligned, or damaged.
- Flue gas system is blocked or leaking.
- Gas-air ratio is incorrect (CO₂ at max output should be ~9.6% for NG, ~10.8% for propane).
- Condensate trap is blocked, preventing proper drainage.
- Diaphragm in the mixer unit is contaminated or torn.
- Heat exchanger is fouled and needs cleaning.
- Gas valve itself is defective.
- Boiler coding card is not seated correctly.

**Immediate checks:** confirm the gas tap is open, check the display for any other codes, and press the reset button. If the fault recurs, a technician should retrieve the last stored fault via service function 6.A and work through the checklist above.
