# Cleaning Schedule

The Bosch Condens 5000 W instructions (6 720 644 143, 2016/04) do not prescribe a separate cleaning schedule distinct from the annual inspection — instead, cleaning is performed on-condition during the yearly service. The following components are cleaned or checked at each annual inspection:

| Component | Interval | Trigger / Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Condensate trap | Annually | Remove, clean sump and hose, refill with ~0.25 L water (section 15.2.8) |
| Heat exchanger | Annually (on condition) | Cleaned if control pressure at max output < 3.9 mbar (0.39 kPa); use brush 1060 and cleaning blade 1061 (section 15.2.5) |
| Burner | Annually (on condition) | Cleaned if fouling or combustion issues found; always re-check gas-air ratio after cleaning (section 15.2.6) |
| Electrodes | Annually | Inspect for dirt; clean or replace as needed (section 15.2.2) |
| Plate-type heat exchanger (ZWB) | On condition | Cleaned/descaled if DHW output drops; use only stainless-steel-approved descaler (grade 1.4401) (section 15.2.4) |
| Strainer in cold water pipe (ZWB) | Annually | Undo pipe and check for debris (section 15.2.3) |
| Diaphragm in mixer unit | Annually | Withdraw from fan intake tube; check for soiling/splits (section 15.2.7) |
| Condensate tray | Annually (with heat exchanger cleaning) | Clean with reversed brush (section 15.2.5) |

Additionally, the siphon-fill programme (service function 4.F) automatically runs for 15 minutes at minimum output whenever the appliance is switched on after being off for 48 hours or more, or when switched from summer to winter mode — this prevents dry-trap flue gas escape.

Source: Bosch Condens 5000 W Installation and Servicing Instructions, document 6 720 644 143 (2016/04).
