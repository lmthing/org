# dhw-capacity

The ZWB 37-2 A combi boiler provides domestic hot water on the instantaneous heating principle. The maximum DHW flow rate is 13.0 L/min (across all gas types). The DHW outlet temperature can be set between 40 °C and 60 °C via the DHW temperature control on the fascia. The specific flow rate per EN 625 (D) is 16.9 L/min.

The maximum permissible cold water supply pressure is 1,000 kPa (10 bar) and the minimum inlet pressure required is 300 kPa (3 bar). The maximum cold water inlet temperature is 60 °C. The boiler can also accept water preheated by solar energy — a solar transfer valve must be fitted upstream and set to 60 °C to prevent excessive temperatures. In accordance with AS 3498, a tempering valve (limited to 50 °C) must be fitted for hot water supplied to sanitary fixtures used for personal hygiene.

The plate-type heat exchanger handles DHW heating, and a strainer in the cold water pipe protects it. The boiler offers Comfort mode (keeps the appliance at the set DHW temperature for near-instant hot water) and Economy mode (only heats water when a tap is opened, saving gas and water).
