# retirement-eligibility

Under current Greek pension law (as reformed by Laws 4387/2016 and 4670/2020), eligibility for a full old-age pension requires:
- At least 40 years of contributions (12,000 days of insurance) for retirement at age 62, OR
- At least 20 years of contributions (6,000 days) with retirement at age 67 (the general age limit).

A reduced (early) pension is available at age 62 with at least 15 years of contributions (4,500 days).
