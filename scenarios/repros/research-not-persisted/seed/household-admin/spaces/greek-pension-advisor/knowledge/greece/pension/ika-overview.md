# ika-overview

IKA (Ίδρυμα Κοινωνικών Ασφαλίσεων — Social Insurance Institute) was historically the largest primary social security fund in Greece, covering private-sector employees. Under recent reforms, IKA was merged into EFKA (Ενιαίος Φορέας Κοινωνικής Ασφάλισης — Unified Social Security Fund), which now administers pensions for virtually all Greek workers.

Dimitris has an IKA membership number (Αριθμός Μητρώου / AM) recorded in his household file as AM 4401982-22. This number identifies his contribution record within the Greek social security system. The AM is the key identifier to use when contacting IKA/EFKA for service history, pension estimates, or application processing.

The Greek state pension is a pay-as-you-go system: current workers' contributions fund current retirees' pensions. Benefits consist of a national (flat) pension plus a contributory (earnings-related) pension. Eligibility and payout depend on years of contributions, age, and lifetime earnings — none of which (beyond the 22-year stamp count) are detailed in the available source documents.
