**Standard IKA/EFKA contact channels (general knowledge):**
- **Main website:** efka.gov.gr — online services include contribution record checks, pension simulations, and application tracking.
- **Phone:** The EFKA unified call centre (1555) handles general inquiries; individual local offices (Peripheral Directorates) serve walk-in visits by appointment.
- **MyEFKALive:** Digital platform for video-call appointments with EFKA officers.
- **Taxisnet login:** Greek social security portals are accessible through the same Taxisnet credentials Dimitris already uses (his VAT no. EL044712883 is noted in the file).
- **Local IKA office:** Athens residents typically fall under the IKA-ETAM Athens Directorate; the specific branch depends on the registered home address (Filolaou 41, 11537 Athens — Kallithea jurisdiction). An appointment can be booked through the EFKA portal.
