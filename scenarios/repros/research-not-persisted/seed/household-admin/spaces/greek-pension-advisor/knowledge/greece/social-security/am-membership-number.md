# am-membership-number

Dimitris's IKA membership is identified by **AM 4401982-22**, as recorded in his household documents (policies.md, under "Pensions & savings — State pension"). In the Greek IKA system, "AM" stands for *Αριθμός Μητρώου* (Registry Number) — the unique identifier assigned to every insured worker. This number is used across all IKA/EFKA interactions: contribution tracking, pension eligibility checks, and benefit claims.

The same document entry confirms **22 years of contribution stamps (ένσημα)** accumulated as of the last update date, 2026-07-12. "Stamps" (ένσημα) are the traditional unit of contribution days; roughly 300 stamps per year of full-time employment, so 22 years represents approximately 6,600 stamps of recorded insured work. Under Greek pension rules, this contribution history is the primary input for calculating both eligibility and payout amount.

The document does not break down earnings history, employer timeline, or specific contribution amounts — only the aggregate years figure is recorded.
