# recent-reforms

Dimitris's household documents do not discuss pension legislation directly. The following is general knowledge about the Greek social security reform framework that affects anyone with an IKA record like AM 4401982-22:

**Katrougalos Law (4387/2016):** Unified all primary pension funds into EFKA. Introduced a single pension calculation formula combining a flat national pension (€384/month at 20 years, adjusted for fewer years) plus a contributory component based on average earnings over the entire working life and a replacement rate. Raised retirement ages toward 67 (or 62 with 40 years of contributions).

**Vroutsis Law (4670/2020):** Increased replacement rates for long careers (over 30 years), introduced a supplementary digital pension file system (ATLAS), and adjusted the accrual rate per contribution year. The national pension amount has since been adjusted by annual ministerial decisions.

**Post-2020 adjustments:** Contribution rates, insurable earnings ceilings, and the treatment of parallel insurance (multiple funds) have been refined through ministerial circulars. For the most current thresholds and rates, EFKA's online pension simulator or a certified accountant/labour lawyer should be consulted.
