In the Greek system, ένσημα (ensima — literally "stamps") are the units of social security contributions credited per days worked. A full year of contributions typically requires roughly 300 working days of stamps, though the exact definition depends on the employment sector and period. The 22-year count represents a significant contribution history — well past the minimum thresholds for eligibility (which under post-2016 reforms require at least 15 years of contributions for a reduced pension at age 62, and 20 years for a full pension at ages 62-67 depending on birth cohort).
