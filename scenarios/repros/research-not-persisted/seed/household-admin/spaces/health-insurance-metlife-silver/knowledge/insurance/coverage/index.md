Coverage details for the MetLife Silver family health insurance policy (MET-SILVER-7). This field splits into three aspects: the type of plan (employer-sponsored group plan), who is covered (family scope — household of four), and what benefits are provided (outpatient visits with a fixed co-pay, emergency access via the card's back line). Every claim is drawn directly from the household policies document.
