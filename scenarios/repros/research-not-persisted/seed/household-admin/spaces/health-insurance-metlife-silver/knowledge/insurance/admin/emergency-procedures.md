# Emergency Procedures

In a medical emergency, the first step is to call the **emergency line printed on the back of the MetLife Silver insurance card**. This line provides immediate guidance and can direct the family to the nearest approved hospital or clinic.

The MetLife Silver plan carries a **€20 co-pay per visit**, which applies to both routine and emergency visits at in-network providers. Keep the insurance card accessible at all times — the emergency number is on it, and hospitals will need the policy number (MET-SILVER-7) and card details at admission.

For non-urgent medical questions, contact the broker Nikoleta (+30 210 555 1182) during business hours.
