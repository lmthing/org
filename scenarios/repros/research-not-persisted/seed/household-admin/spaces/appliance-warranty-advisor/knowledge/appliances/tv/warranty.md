# warranty

The LG OLED55B3 is covered by the manufacturer's **standard 2-year EU warranty**, running from the purchase date **20 November 2024** through **20 November 2026**. As of the last document update (12 July 2026), the TV is **still under warranty**. It will expire on 20 November 2026.

There is no extended warranty, separate insurance policy, or service contract for the TV in any of the household documents on file (policies.md, household-ledger.xlsx). The home insurance policy (Piraeus HomeShield, policy PIR-HOME-882) covers buildings and contents but does not single out the TV for a separate electronics rider in the available records.
