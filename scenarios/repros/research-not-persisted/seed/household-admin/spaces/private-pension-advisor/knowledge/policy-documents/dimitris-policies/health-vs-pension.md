# health-vs-pension

Dimitris has two separate contracts under the MetLife Silver brand, and they serve completely different purposes:

|  | Health insurance | Private pension |
|---|---|---|
| **Policy number** | `MET-SILVER-7` | `MET-PEN-441` |
| **Type** | Group health plan via employer | Personal private pension |
| **Who pays** | Employer (premium covered) | Dimitris (€180/month) |
| **What it provides** | Health cover with €20 co-pay per visit | Projected annuity from age 65 (~2042) |
| **Beneficiary** | Family (health coverage) | Spouse (pension death benefit) |
| **Renewal** | 2027-01-31 | Not recorded in available documents |

These are entirely distinct contracts — confusing them could lead to wrong assumptions about coverage, cost, or beneficiaries. When the household documents list 'MetLife Silver' without a policy number, check the context to determine which contract is being discussed.
