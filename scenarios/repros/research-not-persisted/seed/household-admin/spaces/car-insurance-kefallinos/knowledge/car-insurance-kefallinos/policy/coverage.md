# coverage

The policy (Ασφαλιστήριο Κλάδου Αυτοκινήτου No. 2746423) lists the following coverages with their insured amounts and, where applicable, excesses:

**Liability**
- Bodily injury to third parties and passengers (per victim): €1,300,000
- Property damage to third parties (per accident): €1,300,000
- Cross-liability: €3,500
- Fire liability: €50,000
- Property damage from uninsured vehicle: €50,000

**Own-damage / supplementary coverages**
- Fire (including fire and explosion from terrorist acts, political unrest, or malicious acts): €9,400
- Total theft: €9,400
- Partial theft / damage during theft / theft of fixed parts / theft of non-fixed parts (up to 2% of vehicle value): €9,400
- Natural and geological phenomena damage: €9,400
- Glass breakage (including factory sunroofs): Flexible — 10% of insured value, minimum €1,000, maximum €3,500. Excess: 15% of claim value, minimum €100. Excess is waived if replacement is done at a partner workshop or Glassdrive, ΦΙΛΗΣ Glass, or GlassFit.
- Car rental: Included
- Personal accident (driver): €15,000
- Automatic insured-value adjustment (0.5% monthly reduction): Included
- Total destruction allowance: €6,000
- EU/EEA extension of civil liability: Included
- EU/EEA extension of supplementary coverages: Included
- Accident care (phone 2109310807 or 2109462007): Included
- Roadside and travel assistance (phone 2109310807 or 2109462007): Included
- Legal protection: €3,000
- Preventive health check-up (phone 2109310807 or 2155558888): Included
- Property damage from at-fault vehicle outside friendly settlement: €6,500
- Property damage from at-fault vehicle with foreign plates: €6,500

All coverages are subject to the General and Special Terms of the policy, which were provided to and accepted by the policyholder.
