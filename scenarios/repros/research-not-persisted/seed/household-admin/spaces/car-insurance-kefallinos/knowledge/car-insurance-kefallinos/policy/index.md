Core policy details extracted from the actual Greek car insurance contract (Ασφαλιστήριο Κλάδου Αυτοκινήτου No. 2746423 / 10359487). This field splits the policy into four aspects: full coverage breakdown, premium and renewal dates, insured vehicle data, and contact/assistance information. Use the aspect that matches what the user is asking about — coverage amounts and conditions, premium cost and payment period, vehicle identification and specification, or who to call for claims and roadside assistance. Everything here is sourced directly from the policy document itself.
