This field covers the fundamental legal instrument of property ownership — the title deed. A title deed (συμβόλαιο κυριότητας) is the notarised document that proves ownership of real estate and is registered with the competent Land Registry or Cadastral Office. For the K1K2 plot covered by this space, the deed reference is 44871/2010, registered at the Land Registry of Kallithea. The aspects here explain what a title deed is, how it gets registered, and how ownership can be verified so you know who really holds the property rights. The Filolaou 41 apartment is a separate property with its own mortgage and insurance — it is not covered by this K1K2 deed.
