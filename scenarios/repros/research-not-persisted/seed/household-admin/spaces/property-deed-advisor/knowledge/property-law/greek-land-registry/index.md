Information about the Greek Land Registry (Ktimatologio) as it relates to the K1K2 plot. This field covers the specific Land Registry office in Kallithea where the title deed is registered, how to look up a deed by its number, and what records exist for the K1K2 plot specifically. All details are drawn from the household policies document, which records that the title deed for the K1K2 plot is deed number 44871/2010, registered at the Land Registry of Kallithea.
