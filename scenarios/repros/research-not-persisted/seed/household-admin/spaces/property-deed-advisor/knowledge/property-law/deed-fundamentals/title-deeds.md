# title-deeds

A title deed is the primary legal document that proves ownership of real property. It is drawn up by a notary public (συμβολαιογράφος) and records the transfer of ownership from seller to buyer. The deed includes the full description of the property (location, boundaries, area), the names of the parties, the purchase price or transfer terms, and any encumbrances such as mortgages or easements. For the K1K2 plot, the title deed is numbered **44871/2010** and is registered at the **Land Registry of Kallithea**. The deed is the definitive proof of who owns the plot — without it, no sale, mortgage, or inheritance transfer can proceed. It is separate from the deed for the Filolaou 41 apartment, which has its own mortgage with Piraeus Bank and its own home insurance policy.
