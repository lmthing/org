# registration-process

In Greece, a title deed only becomes fully effective against third parties once it is registered (μεταγραφή) at the competent Land Registry (Υποθηκοφυλακείο) or Cadastral Office (Κτηματολογικό Γραφείο). The notary who drafts the deed is responsible for submitting it for registration. The registry checks the formal requirements and records the deed in its books, creating a publicly searchable chain of title. For the K1K2 plot, registration took place at the **Land Registry of Kallithea** — the office that holds the official ownership records for properties in that municipal district. Registration gives the owner the legal right to sell, mortgage, or transfer the property and protects against competing claims. Anyone can request a copy of a registered deed or a certificate of ownership from the registry, subject to showing a legitimate interest.
