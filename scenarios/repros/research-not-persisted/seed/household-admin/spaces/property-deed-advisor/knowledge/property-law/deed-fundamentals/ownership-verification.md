# ownership-verification

To verify who owns the K1K2 plot, you need to obtain an official certificate or copy of the registered deed from the Land Registry of Kallithea. The key checks are: (1) request a **certificate of ownership** (πιστοποιητικό κυριότητας) showing the current registered owner; (2) request a **certificate of encumbrances** (πιστοποιητικό βαρών) to see whether any mortgages, liens, or claims exist against the property; (3) cross-reference the deed number **44871/2010** against the registry's books to confirm it matches the plot description.
