Natural gas supply for the household at Filolaou 41, Athens. The supplier is Heron Thermansi, delivering gas through the EDA Attikis distribution network. The gas is consumed by a Bosch Condens 5000 W gas condensing combi boiler, which provides both central heating and domestic hot water. The boiler's manufacturer warranty expired in September 2024 and it is now covered only by an annual legal service contract. Billing is separate from boiler maintenance — the supply contract with Heron Thermansi covers gas consumption, while boiler servicing is a distinct responsibility. This field splits on four aspects: the Heron Thermansi supply relationship, the EDA Attikis distribution network, the boiler itself and its maintenance obligations, and the billing records including amounts and due dates.
