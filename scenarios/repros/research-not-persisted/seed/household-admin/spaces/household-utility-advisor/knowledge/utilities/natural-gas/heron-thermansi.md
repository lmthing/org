# heron-thermansi

Heron Thermansi is the natural gas supplier for the household at Filolaou 41, Athens. The supply contract with Heron Thermansi is separate from boiler maintenance — the supplier provides the gas, while the boiler itself is serviced under a different arrangement. Gas is delivered through the EDA Attikis distribution network, meaning Heron Thermansi is the commercial supplier (the retailer), while EDA Attikis owns and operates the physical distribution infrastructure.

The most recent recorded bill is for June 2026, amounting to €58.20, with a payment due date of 2026-07-18.
