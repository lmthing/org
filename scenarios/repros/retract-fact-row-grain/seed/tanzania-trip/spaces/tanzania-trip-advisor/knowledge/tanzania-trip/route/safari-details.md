**Park fees (from the official Ngorongoro Conservation Area tariff document effective at time of writing, and the trip cost spreadsheet):**
- Ngorongoro Conservation Area entry permit: $70.80 per non-EA adult (≥16 years) × 2 = $141.60 (valid 24 hours, single entry).
- Ngorongoro Crater service fee: $70.80 per non-EA adult × 2 = $141.60.
- Ngorongoro Crater vehicle descent fee: $295 per vehicle/trip (non-EA, tare weight up to 2,000 kg).
- Tarangire NP entrance: $59 per person × 2 = $118 (peak season, per spreadsheet).
- Lake Manyara NP entrance: $59 per person × 2 = $118 (peak season, per spreadsheet).
- Total park fees: $814.20.

**Important notes from the briefing material:** Driver-guide Emmanuel mentioned there may be an extra ranger tip expected at the gate — approximately 5,000 TZS, cash, separate from park fees. The material recommends asking Richard at Suricata Safaris about this before paying the safari balance. The Ngorongoro tariff document states crater descent gates close at 16:00 and all visitors must exit by 18:00; only 4×4 vehicles up to 3,500 kg tare weight are permitted on the crater floor; off-road driving is prohibited, and a maximum of 5 vehicles may congregate at any sighting.
