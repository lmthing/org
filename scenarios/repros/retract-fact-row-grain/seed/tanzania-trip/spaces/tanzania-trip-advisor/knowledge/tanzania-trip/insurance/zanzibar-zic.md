# zanzibar-zic

Zanzibar requires all visitors to purchase mandatory travel insurance from the Zanzibar Insurance Corporation (ZIC). This is a separate requirement from any other travel or medical insurance the traveler may already hold — it must be obtained specifically through ZIC. The supplied trip documents confirm this is a mandatory line item in the trip budget, but they do not include the policy document itself. The following details are therefore NOT stated in the available material: the exact policy name or number, whether it covers medical emergencies, evacuation, trip interruption, lost baggage, or personal liability, the claims process, emergency contact numbers, the validity period relative to the traveler's stay in Zanzibar, and whether it is purchased on arrival, online in advance, or through a tour operator.
