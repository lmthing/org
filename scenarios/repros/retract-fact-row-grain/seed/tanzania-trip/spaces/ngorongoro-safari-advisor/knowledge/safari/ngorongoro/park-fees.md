# park-fees

For non-East African citizens (the tariff class that applies to Vasilis and Athina Mari), three charges are due for a one-day Ngorongoro Crater visit. Every figure below is drawn from two real documents: the official NCAA "TARRIFS Visit NGORONGORO for Unforgettable Experiences" tariff sheet and the trip-costs.xlsx spreadsheet that itemizes this trip.

**A. Entry Permit** — $70.80 per adult (age 16+). The PDF lists the non-East African adult entry permit at $70.80. ×2 travelers = $141.60.

**B. Crater Service Fee** — $70.80 per adult (age 16+). The PDF lists the non-East African adult crater service fee at $70.80. ×2 travelers = $141.60.

**C. Vehicle Descent Fee** — $295.00 per vehicle/trip for non-East African registered vehicles. The PDF's Motor Vehicle Permit table lists "Non-East African Citizens — 295.00" for the crater descent weight class (tare weight 2001–3000 kg, the class covering standard safari 4×4 vehicles). The trip-costs spreadsheet confirms this exact figure: "Ngorongoro crater vehicle descent fee (per vehicle/trip, non-EA) — $295."

**Total Ngorongoro-only park fees:** $141.60 + $141.60 + $295.00 = **$578.20**. The trip-costs spreadsheet sums these three line items identically within the broader park-fees category.

These park fees are NOT included in the $1,200 Suricata Safaris package — the package covers guiding, transport, and the two overnight stays (Twiga Camp Site + Jevas Hotel). The park fees are itemized separately in the costs sheet and are presumably paid at the gates or handled by Suricata as part of on-ground arrangements. The PDF also lists other tariff categories the travelers do NOT owe on this trip (public campsite $35.40/adult/night, special campsite $70.80/adult/night, cultural boma entry $20/vehicle, etc.) because they are staying at Jevas Hotel, not camping inside the NCAA, and no cultural boma visit is in the plan.

The PDF notes: "Permit once issued is valid for one entry within 24 hours only" and "Tariffs are subjected to change without prior notice."
