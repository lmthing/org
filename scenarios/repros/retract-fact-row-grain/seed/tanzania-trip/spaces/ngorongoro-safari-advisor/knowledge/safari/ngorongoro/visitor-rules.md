# visitor-rules

Every rule below is quoted or tightly paraphrased from the "VISITOR GUIDELINES AND PROCEDURES" section and the tariff categories of the official NCAA PDF. No rule is invented — if the PDF is silent on a point, that gap is stated.

**Hours and access** — Driving hours are 06:00–18:00; late driving requires permission. Crater descent gates close at 16:00, and visitors must exit the crater floor by 18:00. Permits are single-entry and valid for 24 hours.

**Vehicle requirements** — Only 4×4 vehicles up to 3,500 kg tare weight are allowed on the crater floor (except authorized trucks for organized groups). The speed limit on the crater is 25 kph (general area speed limit is 50 kph).

**Conduct and congestion** — A maximum of 5 vehicles may congregate at any one animal sighting; silence must be maintained. Drivers must keep a safe distance of at least 25 meters from animals. Off-road driving is prohibited. No feeding animals, no littering, no throwing objects to attract attention, no imitating animal sounds or sounding horns. Plastic bags are not allowed in the conservation area. Cigarette packets, lunch boxes, and tissues must be kept in the vehicle for later disposal.

**Guide requirement** — A licensed guide or an official NCAA guide must accompany all crater visitors. (The Suricata Safaris driver-guide Emmanuel fulfills this for Vasilis and Athina Mari.)

**Picnicking** — Picnicking in designated public areas is free. Special picnic areas require advance reservation and payment before arrival.

**Photography and cultural bomas** — Photographing local people is prohibited except in designated Cultural Bomas. Entry to cultural bomas costs $20.00 per vehicle (non-East African rate).

**Camping** — Camping is permitted only in designated areas; special campsites must be reserved and confirmed before arrival. These camping rules do not apply to Vasilis and Athina Mari because they stay at Jevas Hotel.

**Liability** — NCAA provides security but "does not assume any liability for any damage or loss of property or life."

**Penalties** — Over-speeding: TZS 118,000 per vehicle. Off-road driving, wrong parking, and other prohibited actions: TZS 118,000. Fraud: normal fee plus a 100% penalty fine.

The PDF does not state a maximum stay duration on the crater floor beyond the gate-closure times, does not specify a tipping policy for rangers, and does not list any mandatory ranger-tip fee — the ~5,000 TZS ranger tip Emmanuel mentioned at the gate (recorded in the trip journal / voice memo) is an informal gate-level practice, not an NCAA tariff.
