# fees

Tarangire National Park charges an entrance fee of **$59 per person** during peak season for non-East African adults. For two travelers (Vasilis + Athina Mari), this totals **$118** for Tarangire alone.

These park entrance fees are **not** an extra out-of-pocket expense on safari day — they are itemized in the trip cost spreadsheet as part of the overall park-fees budget (alongside Lake Manyara and Ngorongoro fees). The full 3-day Suricata Safaris package costs **$1,200 for two people**, which covers the safari circuit including the campsite lodging (Twiga Camp Site, full board on night 1). A **$240 deposit (20%)** has already been paid; the remaining **$960 balance** is due on arrival in Arusha.

Suricata Safaris' contact Richard (+255 763 222 293, info@suricatasafaris.com) handles the package. Payment of the balance is accepted in **cash (USD, post-2009 notes)** or online via Pesapal at payments.pesapal.com/suricatasafaris with a **+5% surcharge**.

The supplied material does not specify whether Suricata handles the gate payment on the traveler's behalf or whether the travelers pay at the gate and are later reimbursed. Confirm directly with Richard.
