# wildlife

August is the **peak dry season** in northern Tanzania — the best time for wildlife viewing, as animals congregate around permanent water sources.

Tarangire National Park is especially known for:
- **Large elephant herds** — Tarangire has one of the highest elephant densities in Tanzania during the dry season.
- **Iconic baobab trees** dotting the landscape.
- The **Tarangire River**, which runs through the park and acts as a lifeline for wildlife during the dry months, drawing a wide variety of species to its banks.
- General plains game: zebra, wildebeest, giraffe, buffalo, impala, and their attendant predators.

Lake Manyara (day 2) adds a different ecosystem — a groundwater forest, the alkaline lake itself, and the Rift Valley escarpment. It is famous for **tree-climbing lions**, large **flamingo** flocks (seasonal depending on water levels), and **diverse birdlife** (over 400 species recorded).

The supplied material does not provide a species checklist or guarantee specific sightings — wildlife is inherently unpredictable. The description above reflects what is widely documented for these parks in the August dry season.
