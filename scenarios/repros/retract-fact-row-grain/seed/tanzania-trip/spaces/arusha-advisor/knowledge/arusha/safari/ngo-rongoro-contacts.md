# ngo-rongoro-contacts

The Ngorongoro Conservation Area Authority (NCAA) manages the Ngorongoro Crater and surrounding conservation area. The following contact offices are reachable from the Arusha region:

- **Ngorongoro Information Office** — Tel: +255 734 160 014 / +255 734 160 015
- **Karatu Information Office** — Tel: +255 27 253 4068
- **Conservation Commissioner's Office** — P.O. Box 1, Ngorongoro Crater, Arusha – Tanzania. Hotline: +255 27 253 7046. Fax: +255 27 253 7007. Email: cc@ncaa.go.tz. Web: www.ncaa.go.tz

**Key tariff rates for non-East African adults (from the official NCAA tariff schedule):**
- Entry permit (per person, 24 hours, single entry): $70.80 (age 16+); $23.60 (ages 5–16); free (under 5).
- Crater service fee (per person): $70.80 (age 16+); $35.40 (ages 5–16); free (under 5).
- Vehicle descent fee (per vehicle/trip, non-East African): $295.00 (tare weight 2001–3000 kg).
- Public campsite (per person, age 16+): $70.80.
- Special campsite (per person, age 16+): $70.80.
- Walking safari guide fee (per person, non-East African): $23.60 (short walk 1–4 hours); $29.50 (long walk above 4 hours).

**Important rules from the NCAA guidelines:**
- Permits are valid for one entry within 24 hours only. Tariffs are subject to change without notice.
- Only 4×4 vehicles up to 3,500 kg tare weight are allowed on the crater floor.
- Crater descent gates close at 16:00; all vehicles must exit by 18:00.
- Driving hours are 06:00–18:00 unless special permission is obtained.
- Speed limits: 50 kph in the conservation area, 25 kph on the crater floor.
- Plastic bags are prohibited in the Ngorongoro Conservation Area.
- Off-road driving is prohibited; a maximum of 5 vehicles at any animal sighting; keep at least 25 meters distance from animals.
