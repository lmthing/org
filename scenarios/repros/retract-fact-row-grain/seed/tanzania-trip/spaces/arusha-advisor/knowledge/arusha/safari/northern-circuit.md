# northern-circuit

Arusha is the mainland Tanzania gateway and staging town for the northern safari circuit. The 3-day group camping safari runs August 7–9 and visits three protected areas in sequence:

- **Tarangire National Park** — first stop on the circuit; overnight at Twiga Camp Site (full board).
- **Lake Manyara National Park** — second stop; overnight at Twiga Camp Site (full board).
- **Ngorongoro Crater** — final stop; overnight at Jevas Hotel (half board). Descent into the crater floor requires a crater service fee and vehicle descent fee per the NCAA tariff schedule. Ngorongoro nights are cold — warm layers are essential.

The circuit returns to Arusha on August 9, where the post-safari accommodation is at Kutoka Lodge (Aug 9–11). August is peak safari season, ideal for wildlife viewing including the Great Wildebeest Migration corridor.

The supplied material does not detail the exact driving distances or times between each park and Arusha, nor does it specify which specific campsites within Twiga or which route is taken between parks beyond the named sequence.
