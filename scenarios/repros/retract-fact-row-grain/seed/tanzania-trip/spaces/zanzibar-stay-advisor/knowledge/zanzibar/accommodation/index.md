Accommodation across the two distinct Zanzibar bases — the historic Stone Town (Aug 11–13) and the east-coast beach at Kiwengwa (Aug 13–17). Stone Town lodging is at Treasures of Zanzibar House, a property with a characterful interior courtyard featuring white stucco facades, dark wooden balconies, carved railings, and arched windows — confirmed by a courtyard photo in the trip documents. Kiwengwa Beach accommodation is at Ayla Beach House, on the east coast where low tide can limit direct swimming access (plan swim times around tides; the north coast around Nungwi offers more consistent conditions for day trips).
