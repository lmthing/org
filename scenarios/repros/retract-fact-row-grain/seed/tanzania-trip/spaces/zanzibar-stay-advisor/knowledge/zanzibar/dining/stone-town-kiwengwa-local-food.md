## Local Dining in Stone Town and Kiwengwa

### Stone Town
Stone Town offers a rich street-food scene and local restaurants. The Forodhani Gardens night market is a highlight — fresh-grilled seafood, Zanzibar pizza, sugarcane juice, and Urojo soup are all available at affordable prices. Lukmaan Restaurant on Mkunazini Street serves traditional Swahili dishes (biriyani, pilau, curries) all day. Emerson Spice rooftop offers a fine-dining tasting menu with 360-degree Stone Town views (reservations recommended).

### Kiwengwa Beach
Kiwengwa has fewer standalone restaurants; most dining happens at beachfront hotels and resorts. Ayla Beach House itself may offer meals — confirm with the host. Nearby options include:
- **Zan View Restaurant**: rooftop dining overlooking the Indian Ocean, Swahili-Italian fusion.
- **Blue Oyster Hotel Restaurant**: casual beachfront dining, fresh seafood, open to non-guests.
- **Mvuvi Sunset Bar**: relaxed spot on the sand for drinks and light meals at sunset.

### Practical Tips
- Carry cash (TZS) for smaller eateries — many do not accept cards.
- Tap water is not potable; stick to bottled water and avoid ice unless from a trusted source.
- Breakfast arrangements at both Treasures of Zanzibar House and Ayla Beach House should be confirmed with the hosts — neither is explicitly detailed in the trip documentation.
