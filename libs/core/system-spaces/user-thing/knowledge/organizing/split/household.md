# Household admin — split by standing home domain

Splits by each standing DOMAIN of running the home the user would seek advice on — home and contents
insurance, each utility/energy supply, the mortgage or tenancy, and each major appliance or system
(the boiler/heating, etc.). Each is a specialist advising on how that part works: coverage and
providers, tariffs and switching, service intervals and contractors.

The records are DATA: bills, amounts, due dates, payments, meter readings, policy and account
numbers, renewal dates. Never make a "bills", "payments", or "renewals" specialist FOR THOSE ROWS —
but don't let that collapse the domain they belong to. A recurring utility/energy supply is still one
of the standing domains above and still gets its own advisory specialist, even though it has no policy
document of its own to point to (unlike insurance) and even though every fact about it starts out as a
row: "what did we pay last month" is a row; "is there a cheaper option, and how does switching work"
is exactly the kind of question that specialist exists to answer, and whatever it finds out is stored
as ITS knowledge — never skipped for lack of a dedicated source document.
