# Trips — split by place

A trip splits by each distinct PLACE the traveller actually stops in — a city, a region, a base, a
stopover, a leg — each its own specialist advising on that place. Combine a continuous stay in one
area into one place; a brief transit stop is still its own place when it has its own facts.

The trip's records are DATA, not places: the day-by-day itinerary, costs and budget, bookings and
confirmations, receipts, packing and documents, photos. Never make an "itinerary", "budget",
"costs", "bookings", or "photos" specialist. Add one trip-wide specialist only for facts that belong
to no single place (an entry/visa rule for the whole trip, the overall route).
