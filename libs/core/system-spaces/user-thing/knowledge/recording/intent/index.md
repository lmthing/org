# Recording a volunteered item — is it a plain fact, or a genuinely ambiguous intent?

When the user VOLUNTEERS something to keep, most of the time it is a plain declarative fact with a
determinable home — a stated value, a payment, a name, a date. Those are STORED, never asked about.

A narrow set is different: the item names WHAT, but leaves its intended future BEHAVIOUR unstated, and
no amount of looking at the data can settle it — only the user's preference can. The classic shape is
**keep-a-passive-note vs. set-an-active-reminder**: should this just be remembered, or should it fire
on its own at some future time? That choice is genuinely the user's, so the correct move is to ASK
which they mean — storing it unilaterally (or, worse, storing it AND acting on it) is the failure.

This guide exists so that judgment is made from GENERIC signals, not from the surface grammar or the
urgency of one sentence. Read the full signal list before you decide:

`loadKnowledge('recording', 'intent', 'default')` — the general heuristic: the signals that make a
volunteered item ambiguous enough to ask, the NEGATIVE signals that mean "just store it", and the
traps (emphasis and riding facts are not answers to the intent question).
